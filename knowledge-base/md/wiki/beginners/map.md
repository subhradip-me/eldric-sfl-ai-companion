# Travel Map

In the lower-left corner of your screen is the Map, which will let you travel to any of the zones that you have currently unlocked. Click or tap on a zone to travel there instantly, with **no cost or fee of any kind**. In any area other than your home farm, use the arrow keys, WASD, or the pointer at the bottom of your screen to move around and explore the world!

## Home

Your home farm, where most of your time will be spent. You can scroll to view the edges of your land, but cannot walk around like you can in other zones.

## Plaza

The Pumpkin Plaza, this is where you will find many NPCs for deliveries, the Mega Bounty Board, Stella's Megastore, the Potion Room, and two of the keyed treasure chests. The upcoming Pet shop will also be located here.

## Beach (Lvl 4)

Here you will find many other NPCs to make deliveries to, including the master fisher Finn, from whom you can buy rare Fishing Lures. It is also the home of the mysterious Dig Site, where you can unearth many valuable treasures.

## Digging (Lv 4)

This hotspot also takes you to the Beach zone, but at the uppermost entrance right next to Jafar's shop and the Dig Site.

## Retreat (Lvl 5)

The hungry goblins have made their home here, and will accept food in return for valuable FLOWER tokens. This is also the home of Goblet and Garbo, who will turn your trash into treasure, and a powerful goblin who can transport your items and resources across a magic block-chain to be transferred to other farmers.

## Kingdom/Faction (Lvl 7)

Participate in the clash of Factions! Join one of four elite groups and work together to earn Marks and powerful rewards. Once you join a Faction, you will also be able to jump directly to their Faction House whenever you want. There are also royal NPCs here who only accept the finest goods for their deliveries.

## Infernos (Lvl 30)

A volcanic land where a tribe of red-skinned goblins live, the inhabitants of this fiery island trade in sunstone and obsidian, promising enormous benefits in exchange for both.

## Shortcuts

Each link on the map will take you to the entrance to the zone that is closest to the one you are currently in. If you are at your home island, the Retreat, or another zone that doesn't directly link to your destination, you'll end up at the closest entrance relative to the Plaza. This feature can be used to your advantage to jump directly to certain areas.

### Faction Shop

Want to save some steps before cashing in those hard-earned Marks? Teleport to the Digging Site, then walk to the right into the Kingdom and you are there.

### Raven/Tywin

To make a delivery to either of these troublemakers, teleport to the Kingdom, then walk downward toward the Plaza. You will be in the upper-left corner of the map.

### Minigame Portal

If you are lucky enough to be in the Bumpkins Faction, teleport to the Faction House, then walk out the front door to be right in front of the portal. Nightshades also have a shorter walk from their House.

### Gambit

This solitary goblin can be reached easily by teleporting to the Kingdom and walking up to him.

### Bert/Timmy/Betty

If the trouble in the Woodlands ever subsides, the path to these three NPCs will open back up: teleport to the Woodlands, then walk left into the Plaza. Until then, this shortcut is purely historical.
