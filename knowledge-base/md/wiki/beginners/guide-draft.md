# Beginner's Guide (90.69%)

Sunflower Land is designed as a journey. From a humble farmer planting seeds to a master explorer unlocking rare resources, crafting advanced items, and shaping the economy. This guide serves as your compass in exploring the vast world of Sunflower Land. 

**For returning players** please read the [recap section](/en/beginners/guide-draft#lxix-returning-players) first.

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/gamerules.png)

# I. EARLY GAME

Your journey begins after [creating a Farm](/en/home/start). Learn and explore the basics on the Basic/Tutorial Island. The upper-left menu (Bumpkin portrait) now has icons for the Calendar and the Chapter Dashboard.

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/lv1-new-farm.png)

A newly created farm.

## 1\. Tools & Gathering Wood & Minerals

Gathering resources is your first task. Start chopping down Trees for Wood using the Axe tool provided to you. As you advance, you'll be able to mine Minerals (Gold, Iron, and Stone). The right tool is needed to harvest each kind of resource node. Tools are consumables (ex. you lose 1 Axe each time you chop down 1 Tree) thus you're going to craft more Tools at Blacksmith's Workbench for future gathering.

| ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-blacksmith'sworkbench.png)<br>Blacksmith's Workbench |
| --- |

## 2\. Land Expansion

You can expand your land to increase space and discover more Resource nodes once you've acquired the expansion materials required. Completing an expansion will instantly regenerate most Resource Nodes (Trees, Stone, Iron, Gold, Crimstone and Oil Wells only). You can use Gems to instantly complete the Expansion time (Basic/Tutorial and Petal Island only).

| ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-expansion.png)<br>Expansion button. | ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-completeexpansion.png)<br>Completed expansion. |
| --- | --- |

Resources needed for [future expansions.](https://sfl.world/info/expansion)

## 3\. Seeds & Growing Crops

After finishing your initial Land Expansion you'll be able to plant and harvest crops by unlocking the Crop Plots. You can buy more seeds and sell your harvested crops for Coins at Betty's Market. It will always be Spring Season on the  Basic/Tutorial Island which only allows you plant crops/produce limited for Spring Season. You'll unlock the full four-seasons once you upgrade to Petal Island. 

| ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-betty's_market.png)<br>Betty's Market. |
| --- |

## 4\. Cooking & Leveling

After finishing your second Land Expansion, you discover the Fire Pit ([a type of Cooking Building](/en/mechanics/cooking)). You can now cook and prepare food to feed and level up your Bumpkin. For additional food options, construct the other Cooking Buildings from the Blacksmith's Workbench Build Tab.

| ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-firepit.png)<br>Fire Pit. | ![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/home-feedbumpkin.png)<br>Feed via Main Bumpkin. |

## 5\. Deliveries & World Map

Delivery is a system where NPCs request specific items (Crops, Resources, Food, Flowers and Crafted items) in exchange for rewards like Coins, $FLOWER, and Chapter Tickets. You can ignore Chapter Ticket deliveries at this stage.

To complete a delivery, travel through the World Map to various locations (Plaza, Beach, Kingdom and Retreat) and interact with the corresponding NPC. The first 15 deliveries refresh automatically while deliveries from the 16th onward reset once daily at 00:00 UTC. Each day, a new delivery is assigned for each NPC.

However keep in mind that not all deliveries are profitable nor worth completing. You can skip a delivery if it was assigned to you on the previous day.

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/map-world.png)

World Map with NPC locations and relevant buildings.

On the Retreat Map, the Goblin Raffle is also defunct.

More info regarding [NPC Delivery.](/en/mechanics/deliveries)

## 6\. Water Well & Plot Fertility

Some Crop Plots appear dried up when you discover them which makes them unusable for planting. Building a Water Well gives them their plot fertility that enables you to plant crops on them. You need to upgrade your Water Well as you unlock more Crop Plots.

## 7\. Currency / Coupons

There are plenty of in-game currency which might confuse you at first. Below is a list that coves most of the currencies you would encounter.

The primary currencies:

*   **$FLOWER** - main Token of Sunflower Land; Used for purchasing at the Market Place; $FLOWER Auctions.
*   **Gems** - used for Gem Restocks, VIP purchase, Land Expansions Cost, Gem Auctions.
*   **Coins** - used to buy Seeds & Tools; Land Expansion and Building Cost, Potion Room.

Some supplementary currencies:

*   **Love Charms** - can be exchanged to $FLOWER (50 LC=1 $FLOWER); Used to buy at the Love Shop.
*   **Marks** - used for purchasing at Eldric (Faction Shop).
*   **Emblems** \- used to boost the Marks earned in Faction Tasks.
*   **Chapter Tickets** - currently Bracelets (Chapter 11); Used for Ticket Auctions and purchasing at Stella's Shop.
*   **Potion Tickets** \- used for purchasing at Eins' Potion Shop.

More info regarding [in-game currency.](/en/home/tokens)

## 8\. Restocking

The Daily Shipment provides only a small number of basic/medium seeds and tools. Skills do not affect the Daily Shipment. Restocking the Market, Workbench or Treasure Shop requires Gems as payment. Using Gems for restocks is an intended part of the game’s progression.

## 9\. Skills & Specialization

Skills gives you the ability to enhance your farming experience by providing a multitude of boosts and quality of life functions. You gain one Skill point per Bumpkin level increase. Allocate your skill points carefully and strategically especially at this stage.

You can redistribute your skill points via the skill reset. Each player receives a free skill reset then the next one becomes available after 180-days. If you want to reset your skills before the 180-day cooldown period, you'll have to spend some Gems. Gem cost increases incrementally after each Gem reset.

More info regarding [Skills and Specialization.](/en/mechanics/skills)

## 10\. Limited Market Place Access

You can purchase crops, resources, power-ups, and cosmetic items at the Marketplace. However, selling items (for $FLOWER) is still locked at this stage.

More info regarding the [Marketplace](/en/mechanics/marketplace).

## 11\. Reputation 

Increase your Reputation to unlock more game features (Market-Place Listings and Offers, $FLOWERS Deliveries and more).

More info regarding [Reputation.](/en/mechanics/reputation)

## 12\. Minting of Farm / Store on Chain

Minting your Farm turns your land into an NFT and registers it on the blockchain, granting you access to blockchain transactions.

To mint your farm, you must have POL for the gas fee and reach Seedling [Reputation](/en/mechanics/reputation) (250 reputation points). Go to the Retreat and head north-east and look for the the Goblin with the Wallet Icon. 

After your farm is minted, you can now withdraw and deposit wearables, collectibles, and in-game items. A minted farm is required for $FLOWER withdrawal but is not required for $FLOWER deposit. The "Mint Your Farm" button will be replaced to the "Store On-Chain" option once minting is complete.

Store on chain saves your game progress to the the blockchain and resets the Hoarding Limit. You'll be prompted to store on chain (requires POL for gas fee) when you reach a hoarding limit.

## 13\. Proof of Humanity / Verification

This protocol is in place to prevent abuse of system via multi-accounting and botting. The face scans do not store your face an image/selfie but only as a graphical representation.

## 14\. Daily Chest

The Daily Chest provides free items and will be shown to you after you log in for the first time after game reset. If you don't want to open it right away, you can close the window and find it again at the Mailbox on your Farmhouse. You can also see the upcoming rewards provided that you maintain a daily streak.

More info regarding the [Daily Chest.](/en/mechanics/chests#daily-chest)

## 15\. VIP Access

Getting the VIP Access has many perks which include: 

*   +10% EXP Boost
*   Cooking Building queue (Set a food to begin after the current one finishes)
*   Crafting Box queue
*   Multi-Cast fishing (Use multiple rods in one go)
*   Additional $FLOWER Deliveries
*   600 Reputation points (Enough to remove all marketplace restrictions immediately)
*   VIP-only Marketplace Purchases
*   Chapter-exclusive bonus
*   VIP Reward Milestones in Chapter Track
*   Discount on expansion costs
*   Bonus daily food item

## 16\. Farm Collectibles

Collectibles are supply limited cosmetics and items to boost your farm. These can be found in your inventory and be placed in your land. Duplicates of collectibles with boost don't stack. [Some Boosts don't stack](/en/faq#which-sfts-do-not-stack)

More info regarding [Collectibles.](/en/sfts)

## 17\. Bumpkin Wearables

Wearables are fashionable cosmetic & powerful outfits for your Bumpkin. These can be equipped on the Equip tab when you click your Bumpkin Profile or your Farmhands. Duplicates of wearables with boost don't stack.

More info regarding [Wearables.](/en/sfts)

## 18\. Bud NFTs

Buds have sprouted from the ground and these unique collectibles can give buffs for your farm. Buds are bought at the Marketplace and can rarely be won in a special Chapter Raffle.

More info regarding [Buds](/en/mechanics/buds)

## 19\. Pets NFT & Common

Hatch a friendly creature to care for on your farm! Pets eat different foods every day, which gives them the energy to bring you valuable items. These treasures can be used to build Shrines that provide week-long buffs, and as bait to catch rare Crustaceans. Or you can just sell them to other players.

NFT Pets are unique creatures that can do more than common ones, but must be bought or won in an auction. Common pets are more than sufficient for most players.

More info regarding [Farm Pets](/en/mechanics/pets)

## 20\. Kingdom Mini Games

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/npc-billy.png)

Billy (Mini-games)

You can access the Kingdom at Level 7. It may be too early to join a Faction at this stage but you can start playing from Billy's Mini Games to earn Marks for free. You can claim Marks once daily in  the mini-game when you complete the objective or target score. Emblems do not affect/multiply the Marks earned in the mini-games.

## 21\. Landscaping Mode and Biomes

You can rearrange and decorate your farm using the Landscaping Mode. Biomes are “skin” to change your farm’s appearance but only the Prestige you reached can be purchased.

More info regarding Landscaping Mode.

Fly to the Love Island via the heart Balloon (located at the Plaza) and complete the Puzzle to earn a Bronze Love Box.

# II. MID GAME

You only got a taste of what to expect from the Early game. Keep building your farm and acquire more experience as you progress to the Petal and Desert Island.

## 28\. Petal Island

You unlock additional resources (Fruits, Flowers+Beehive, Crimstone and Sunstone) and more game mechanics by upgrading to Petal Island. You also get the Blossombeard (+10% EXP) as a bonus.

## 29\. Four-Seasons & Unknown Events

Every 7-days a new season will appear (Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter). 

It will take 14-days after you the upgrade before your in-game calendar synchronizes with the Global Events.

Unknown Events could bring a Positive Event (buff) or Negative Event (debuff) to your Farm. To protect your Island from Negative Weather Events, go to Bailey at the Plaza and buy the weather protection items that should be on your Land before any Unknown Events.

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/infog-unknown_event_101.png)

More info regarding [Unknown Events.](/en/mechanics/seasons#calendar)

## 30\. Seasonal Crops, Fruits and Flowers & Beehive

Pay attention to the current season. Each type of produce can only be planted during its designated season. For more information, check Betty’s Market Guide. Seeds that are out of season cannot be bought.

You can now grow Fruits on the Fruit Patch. To plant a new Fruit, harvest the newly discovered Apple Tree several times until it withers. You then can chop it down to clear the Fruit Patch for replanting.

You can also start planting Flowers and harvesting Honey. You can experiment with different flower combinations to produce specific Flowers. The Beehive fills only when a Flower is planted. A Bee Swarm gives your current crops a +0.2 yield. This can occur (10% chance) when a full Beehive(at 100%) is harvested. 

More info regarding [Flowers.](/en/mechanics/flowers)

## 31\. Discover the Magical Gems

Along with wood and minerals, you can now mine Magical gems (Crimstone and Sunstone). Mining Crimstones for five consecutive days gives a bonus yield on the fifth day. A Sunstone rock can only be mined up to ten times before it disappears forever but it has a special use case.

## 32\. Infernos & Sunstone

Infernos is a location in the World Map (Lv30 Required) not to be confused with Volcano Island/Prestige. Here you can exchange your mined Sunstones for additional Resource nodes from Gunther.

More info regarding [Infernos/Sunstone.](/en/mechanics/sunstone)

## 33\. Composters

![](/user-pages/jaepi/guide-uwu/faq_composter_overview_chart.webp)

Composters are buildings (from the Workbench) that produce Fertilizers and Bait/Worm and it will require resources in order for it to run.

Only one type of crop fertilizer (Sprout Mix or Rapid Root) can be used at a time. 

## 34\. Fishing & Crustaceans

Fishing will require you to have Fishing Rods (from Workbench), a Bait (produced by a Composter) and Chum (optional). Fishing gives either a Fish or Treasure/Item. Fish can be used in Deliveries/Mega Bounty Board or be eaten for XP. Some Fish deliveries are also not worth the effort and cost to complete. 

Make sure to build the Fish Market to turn your catches into special processed items. These products are used to cook special seafood dishes, and can also be used as bait to ensure the next fish you catch will be exactly the one you want.

You can maximize your fishing gains by fishing during Fishing Frenzy Days. You can also slowly complete the Fishing Codex Achievements for the Wearables and Collectibles with Boost.

The fishing pier on your island is the perfect place to place Crab Pots. Fill them with rare items as chum and passively attract special sea creatures. Use these Crustaceans for deliveries, bounties, or to cook powerful foods.

More info regarding [Fishing.](/en/mechanics/fishing)

## 35\. Animals & Petting Tools

You can build the Hen House and/or the Barn to start taking care of Animals. Animals are Skill and Boost dependent especially Cows and Sheep since they can eat up plenty of your crops.

How-to-animals:

*   You buy an animal and they are hungry by default.
*   Make their food using the Feeder Machine (Corn, Wheat and Barley required).
*   Feed them preferably with their favorite food for more animal XP.
*   They will level up and go to sleep once their XP bar is full.
*   They will ask the use of petting tools while they sleep (this is optional and only gives bonus animal XP).
*   Once they are awake then you can collect their produce and they become hungry once again.
*   Beware of animal sickness as it can spread to the healthy animals. Use Barn Delight Medicine to cure them.
*   Complete weekly animal bounties by trading your animals for rewards (sick animals only earn 75% of the reward)

More info regarding [Animals.](/en/mechanics/animals)

Beds employ farm hands. It will be +1 farm hand  for every unique bed on your farm. Your Main Bumpkin and Farm hands can be equipped with wearables. If you have more than 1 wearable with boosts, you can equip other farm hands with your additional wearables so that those boosts apply to your farm.

## 40\. Intro to Factions

You can pledge your allegiance to one of the four Factions: Bumpkins, Goblins, Nightshades, or Sunflorians. All Factions share the same mechanics: 

*   Eldric’s Faction Shop access
*   Faction Pet and XP Boost perks
*   Faction Kitchen
*   Weekly Faction War and it's rewards

The main differences between them are their names, Faction Gear designs, and overall vibe.  

Joining a Faction requires paying a [Membership Fee](/en/faq#how-to-join-factions). You may [leave your current Faction](/en/faq#can-i-leave-the-faction-how-to-leave-the-faction) to join another, but doing so will require paying the another Membership Fee and incur a 6-week penalty of no Faction Pet XP boost.

Marks serve as the primary Faction currency that is earned and spent within Faction-related activities. You can use Marks at Eldric’s Faction Shop to purchase Wearables, Collectibles, Temporary Boosts, Food, and Keys. 

Emblems provide a multiplier boost for Marks earned on Faction Pet Feeding and the Faction Kitchen. It can be bought at the Market Place.

Players typically join a Faction early to gain the Faction Pet’s EXP Bonus. To become a contributing member, you’ll need to have all Cooking Buildings and Crafting Box.

## 41\. Intro to Chapter Race: Chapter Tickets and Tasks

Chapters are recurring three-month-long events in Sunflower Land. You can complete various tasks to earn Chapter Tickets and Chapter Points.

Tasks that reward Chapter Tickets include:

*   Daily Login Reward- 1 Ticket
*   Ticket Deliveries- 5 Points per Ticket
*   Weekly Chores- 3 Point per Ticket
*   Animal Ticket Bounties- 5 Points per Ticket
*   Poppy’s Mega Bounty Board- 5 Points per Ticket

Things you can use Tickets for:

*   Entries into the weekly Raffles- 10 entries per ticket, plus special Chapter Raffle Tickets worth 1 entry each
*   Bid in the auctions that take place near the end of the Chapter (Auction Week)
*   Buy items at Stella's Megastore

Each Chapter features its own exclusive Stella's Store Items, Chapter Tickets, Digging Treasures, Marine Marvels and Mutants (Rare Flowers, Cows, Sheep, and Chickens). They are only obtainable during their own specific Chapter. Once a new Chapter begins, the remaining previous Chapter Tickets and Digging Treasures cannot be used anymore but they still can be sold for Coins. 

You do not need to complete every task at this stage. Many tasks can be costly so prioritize the ones you can manage while continuing to build your farm. You can instead use this time to experience the flow of how things work to better prepare for upcoming Chapters.

More info regarding [Chapters.](/en/mechanics/chapters)

## 42\. Mutants

Mutants are Chapter-exclusive collectibles that randomly spawn when you do certain tasks. Mutants can either be a boost or a cosmetic. 

List of mutants and how to get them:

*   Mutant Chicken - Harvesting Chicken
*   Mutant Cow - Harvesting Cow
*   Mutant Sheep - Harvesting Sheep
*   Mutant Flower - Harvesting Flower
*   Chapter Fish - Fishing during Full-Moon

The chance of obtaining a mutant is VERY small. Don't be discouraged if you go several chapters regularly growing and ranching and never find one. They can always be bought from the Marketplace after the chapter is over.

Mutant Crops are not Chapter-bound collectibles that are harvested from Magic Beans (Potion House). There are only three Mutant Crops (Potent Potato, Radical Radish and Stellar Sunflower), all of which can be bought at the Marketplace also.

## 43\. Eins & Potion Room

Eins' Potion Room requires Coins to play and is where you earn Potion Tickets. You can exchange the Potion Tickets for Magic Beans, Lab-Grown Collectibles and Exotic Crops. 

More info regarding [Potion Room.](/en/mechanics/minigames/Potion-House)

## 44\. Desert Prestige

You unlock Oil and Oil-dependent Buildings (Green House and Crop Machine) by upgrading to Desert Island. You also get the Desert Gnome (+10% Cooking Speed) as a bonus. You can no longer use Gems to instantly complete the Expansion time.

## 45\. Oil, Greenhouse and Crop Machine

You can now harvest Oil. It is not a tradeable resource. Each harvest yields 10 Oil, with a bonus of +20 Oil on every third drill (10, 10, 30 pattern). You can also build the Greenhouse and Crop Machine. These buildings require Oil to operate so it can be costly to operate without the proper Skills and Boosts.

The Greenhouse allows you to grow 2 different types of crops (Rice, Olive) and 1 fruit (Grape). I will require Oil to grow them:

*   Grape - 3 Oil per seed
*   Rice - 4 Oil per seed
*   Olive - 6 Oil per seed

Crop Machine automates your crop production for certain Basic and Medium Crops. You now have the option of not manually planting thousands of Sunflower. You can unlock additional crop options for the Crop Machine through Machinery Skills.

More info regarding [Greenhouse](/en/mechanics/harvesting#greenhouse) and Crop Machine.

## 46\. Faction War participation

Faction is a burn-to-earn Marks system. So before you participate, it’s recommended to have Emblems (at least a +300% Multiplier Rank) to maximize your rewards. The long-term goal is to acquire exclusive Faction Gear such as the Faction Shield, Faction Quiver or Medallion.  
  
Join the Faction War to complete faction tasks (Pet Feeding and Faction Kitchen), climb the leaderboard, and earn Marks. The Faction War ranks each Faction based on the total Marks accumulated by their Top 100 members. Weekly leaderboard rewards are given to the Top 100 members of each Faction.  The winning Faction will also receive a bonus +10% Marks based from their contribution.

More info regarding [Factions.](/en/factions)

# LXIX. Returning Players

Sunflower Land has evolved far beyond a simple farming simulator. With this evolution come new mechanics, gameplay updates, and the retirement of some older features. This section gives a quick recap for returning players before diving back into the full guide.

Changes relating to Tokens and currency:

*   Token Changes: SFF > SFL token (in Polygon Network) > $FLOWER token (in Base Network and Ronin Network).
*   SFL that are in-game were automatically converted to $FLOWER.
*   Remaining Block Bucks in your inventory can be sold for Gems at Garbo (Retreat).
*   Buying Seeds and Tools: Pol (ex-Matic) > Block Bucks > Coins
*   Restocking Seeds and Tools: Pol (ex-Matic) > Block Bucks > Gems
*   Buying Farm Hands using Block Bucks (Farmhand coupon) > now Crafting Beds to add Farmhand.

Integration of new game mechanics:

*   Bumpkins & Bumpkin Levels. If you can’t find your House/Tent or Bumpkin look for it in your Basket/Inventory.
*   Chapters
*   Four-Seasons, Seasonal Crops, Fruits and Flowers
*   Land Expansion & Prestige
*   Grub Shop is replaced by NPC Delivery
*   Crab Pot stations near the fishing pier
*   Salt Farm next to fishing pier

Some game mechanic changes and discontinuation:

*   Only 1 Hen House is required. You can sell the extra Hen House and Legacy Chickens at Garbo.
*   Only 1 Water Well structure is required. You can sell the extra Water Well at Garbo.
*   The Tent was discontinued and can be sold at Garbo.
*   The Wishing Well is discontinued, but you can still get rewards from liquidity positions from a link in the Marketplace Dashboard.
*   Bert's Obsession and Poppy's Request were merged to form Poppy's Mega Board.
*   Goblin Exchange was discontinued to prioritize the use of the Market Place.
