# FAQ

## How much money will I earn/how many flowers will I make per week/how to profit/etc?

The short answer: **Nothing**. This game is not a source of easy income or a money making scheme. What it is, is a game that allows you to keep the value of investments that you make into it. With most games, you can spend money on items or powerups, but after you get some utility out of it in the game, your money is essentially gone. In Sunflower Land, you retain your investments, and can sell off resources or items later and reclaim their value. You can read more about the developer's vision here: [Healthy Gaming](https://docs.sunflower-land.com/project/healthy-gaming)

The long answer is that after investing a **large** amount of time, effort, and _real money_ (existing capital) into the game; building up your farm, leveling your character, and buying expensive resource-boosting items, it is possible to get your income to self-sustaining levels, allowing you to pay for your VIP subscription, sfl.world notification bot, and regular expenses for resources, entirely from the FLOWER you earn from deliveries and trading resources. Progressing further may allow you to set aside some FLOWER from your earnings to trade for fiat currency, but it is strongly recommended that you just use the money you earn to re-invest back into the game and grow your farm even further.

## When Do Deliveries Reset?

NPC deliveries reset time is at 00:00 UTC. (Note: You get a new delivery if the previous one is completed.)

## Help! I ran out of coins and can't buy more seeds.

Don't worry, this is not as bad as it seems. Sunflower seeds cost 0.01 coins, and can be sold for 0.02. Slow going, but you can get back on your feet this way. If you literally have zero coins and cannot sell any resources or treasures of any kind, don't panic.  
Visit the Plaza and find any player, then travel to their farm (magnifying glass icon). Follow the prompts to clean their farm, then take the weeds and dung you collected to Garbo at the Goblin Retreat to make a few Coins. You can do this five times per day by default.  
While you're waiting, collect the mushrooms that appear on your farm periodically, and make them into Mushroom Stew at the Firepit. Wait until one of the Goblins requests some, then use the FLOWER to buy crops and sell them for coins.  
If you can't access the Retreat, or buy resources on the Marketplace just yet, simply wait a few days until your daily reward comes up Coins or the Mega Reward, and you will be back to farming before you know it.

## What is a bee swarm?

Once your Bee Hive is at 100%, when you collect honey from it, there is a 10% chance of summoning a Bee Swarm which gives all of your crops a +0.2 boost. There are skills to increase the chance of a swarm and the amount of the boost. This is on top of any fertilizers and multiple swarm bonuses will stack. Read more about [flower gardening](/en/mechanics/flowers).

## What is Full Moon?

Full moon is a special day that occurs once in 4 weeks wherein there are increased chances of catching Marine Marvels. The Full Moon Day also comes with 3 special moon fruit seeds (you can only buy 1 of each at Betty) and if you plant them, you can use those fruits in the lava pit (volcano island), sell them on the marketplace or sell to Betty for coins. In addition, if the season is Summer and you have Eggplant growing, they will all instantly mature at reset time.

## What are moon berries?

Moon berries are 3 special moon fruit seeds (you can only buy 1 of each at Betty on a Full Moon Day) and if you plant them, you can use those fruits in the lava pit (volcano island), sell them on the marketplace or sell to Betty for coins. They can also be used for the Lunar Doll crafting item. Although you can only buy the seeds during a Full Moon Day, you can plant and harvest them at any time. Read more about the [Full Moon](/en/mechanics/seasons#full-moon).

## What is Sunstone?

Sunstone is a precious non-tradeable resource. You can mine each sunstone node 10 times (needs a golden pickaxe). It takes 3 days for the sunstone to reset so that you can mine it again. Each mine gives you 1 Sunstone. Once you are done mining 10 sunstones, the node will disappear. The sunstones you mined, can be exchanged at Infernos Island (unlocked at level 30) for additional resource nodes that you can place on your farm. (Like tree/fruit plot/stone/iron etc.)  Read more about [Sunstone](/en/mechanics/sunstone).

## I am on Petal Paradise and I have no double delivery? / No Fish frenzy etc.?

You are in a separate calendar for the initial 2-week period when you upgrade, where you have different events than the rest of the players. After the 2-week period, you join the main calendar and your seasons sync with the rest of the players. Please see your in-game calendar to assess the events.

## Where to by pumpkin seed?

Pumpkin is an autumn-only seed. If you are on 2nd island, you can plant it in the Autumn season. On basic island it is perpetually Spring, so you will only be able to purchase pumpkin off of the marketplace.

## How to do delivery?

You need to go to where the respective NPCs are located and deliver the items. If you click on an NPC delivery, it will show you where they are located. Example: Raven is located at Pumpkin Plaza, so you need to head there and look for her. Read more about [deliveries](/en/mechanics/deliveries).

## How to decide what food to cook?

If I am looking to level up quick, I will sacrifice cost and go for high XP food and cook that. If I am slowing the leveling, I will prefer cost-efficient food. Every food is useful in some way, though. Read more about [cooking](/en/mechanics/cooking).

## What to do with my second hen house?

If you have more than 1 Hen House, you can sell your second Hen House to [Garbo](/en/npcs/garbo). Game mechanics have changed. Now chickens are housed inside the Hen House and the building can be levelled up to house more chickens.

## What is the use of Farm Hands? / Should I craft Beds?

Beds help employ farm hands. You can appoint 1 farm hand for every 1 unique bed on your farm. (Meaning you cannot just build a second Basic Bed) If you have more than 1 wearable with boosts, you can equip other farm hands with your additional wearables so that those boosts apply to your farm.   
(Example: if you have carrot amulet + beetroot amulet, then by equipping one farm hand with carrot amulet and the other farm hand with beetroot amulet, both boosts can apply to the farm.) Read more about Farmhands[farmhands](/en/mechanics/farmhands).

## How to join factions?

There are 4 factions to choose from. To join a faction, you need to be a level 7 or above. You meet that criterion; the Kingdom becomes accessible. Once you decide on which faction you'd like to join, you can walk up to the NPC in front of their HQ in the Kingdom, pay the joining fee and join the faction. Read more about [Factions](/en/factions).

The joining fee is based on the faction leaderboard placements from the previous week.   
1st Place: 50 Flower   
2nd Place: 30 Flower   
3rd Place: 10 Flower   
4th Place: 5 Flower

## Can I leave the faction? How to leave the faction?

Yes, it is possible to leave a faction. You need to sell all of your emblems (if any). You will also need to use up your marks that you earned in that faction or you will lose them once you leave. After you have sold off the emblems and used up your marks, head to the NPC inside your faction where you can see emblems, and scroll to the very end till you see a leave faction button. (Ref. image.) Click on it and leave the faction.

Please note: To join another faction, you will once again need to pay the joining fee. Even if you join another one right away, you will be unable to get an XP bonus from the faction pet for six weeks.

## I cannot buy a music box, will my chickens die? Animals are so expensive what to do?

It's not mandatory to use the music box, you can feed the chicken when it wakes up, only difference being it requires a little more food to compensate for the music box xp. Animals are not recommended for new players as they are difficult to sustain. What you can do is bring the chickens to the required bounty level and sell them off.

## When does the Gift Giver appear?

Gift givers are real players, admins / devs who appear anywhere in the game at any given time of their choosing and the players who spot them and interact, get a present, so... they have no set time.

## Where are fruit plots? I just upgraded to Petal Paradise.

If you have recently acquired fruit plots, they will have apple trees planted automatically. You need to harvest the trees fully (3 - 5 harvests) post which the tree will die. You need an axe to dig out that dead tree stump after which you can plant a new fruit. (Get seeds from Betty in your farm, where you get all other crop seeds.) Read more about [fruit farming](/en/mechanics/harvesting#fruit).

## How to participate in Goblin Raffle?

The Goblin Raffle has been merged into the Chapter Raffle system. Visit the Prizes box in the Auctioneer's house and look for the raffle that uses the Prize Ticket.

## Do SFTs stack?

Can you get more than one of the same item and combine their bonuses? No, every SFT will provide one boost no matter how many of them you own. Different items that provide the same bonus will stack, though (Desert Rose and Chicory both provide +10% additional flower chance).

In addition, some items are incompatible with one another and will cancel out a lower bonus:

1.  Angel Wings and Devil Wings are different items but have the same bonus so only one will work at a time
2.  Karkinos is disabled if Cabbage Boy is present
3.  Nancy is disabled if Scarecrow or Kuebiko are present
4.  Scarecrow is disabled if Kuebiko is present
5.  Woody the Beaver is disabled if Apprentice Beaver or Foreman Beaver are present
6.  Apprentice Beaver is disabled if Foreman Beaver is present
7.  Faction helmet is burned to obtain the Faction Crown; if bought again, the helmet is only cosmetic

## What do Guardians do?

The guardians double positive events and protect the farm against disasters. Example: If it's Bountiful Harvest and you get +1 crop, with the Summer guardian, it will become +2. This applies to the Fish Frenzy (+2 Fish), Sunshower (75% faster crop plot speed), and Full Moon (+200% chance for Marine Marvel map pieces) events also. The only thing it does not affect is Double Delivery Day.

## What does the Chef Apron do?

If a goblin is asking for a cake and paying you 1 $Flower for it and you have a Bumpkin / Farm Hand equipped with the Chef Apron, then the goblin will pay you 1x120% = 1.2 $Flower for it.

## I tried to withdraw $Flower and now that transaction is stuck and I cannot claim sales from marketplace.

You need to push that stuck transaction by submitting it on chain. (Please ensure you have ETH on base to cover gas fees.) Once that transaction goes through, the marketplace claim issue will resolve.

## What are the Composters?

They are buildings you can build and place on your farm. Each composter gives a different type of fertilizer and Bait. Refer image.

![](/faq/6.png)

| **Composter Type** | **Compost Bin** | **Turbo Composter** | **Premium Composter** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Unlocks at** | Level 7 | Level 12 | Level 18 |
| **Building Cost** | 5 Wood<br>5 Stone | 50 Wood<br>25 Stone | 50 Gold |
| **Composting Cost** | **Varies based on Season** | **Varies based on Season** | **Varies based on Season** |
| **Type of Bait that it gives**<br>(Used in Fishing) | Earthworm | Grub | Red Wiggler |
| **Type of Fertilizer that it gives**<br>(Used for Crop Plots & Fruit Patches) | Sprout Mix<br>(Increases crop yield from plots by **+0.2**) | Fruitful Blend<br>(Boosts yield from each fruit growing on fruit patches by **0.1**) | Rapid Root<br>(Reduces crop growth time from plots by **50%**) |

Composters require time to process, but this can be decreased by adding eggs (feathers with the Feathery Business skill). The reduction time is fixed (i.e. 2 hours), and if the composter has less time than you would deduct from the boost, it will be completed immediately.
