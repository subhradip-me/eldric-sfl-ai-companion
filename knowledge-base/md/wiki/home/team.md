# 👨‍🌾 Meet the Sunflower Land Team

## 🌱 Our Origins

Sunflower Land began as **Sunflower Farmers**, a side project that went viral in January 2022 – becoming so popular it actually broke the Polygon network for a week! What started as three friends building for fun quickly evolved into a full-time mission thanks to an incredible community that demanded more.

This passion project grew into **Thought Farm Pty Ltd**, a Sydney-based company dedicated to building "the world's #1 Community Built Web3 Farming Game."
