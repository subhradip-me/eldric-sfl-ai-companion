## Coins

Coins are a non-tradeable in-game token that is used to purchase tools, seeds, buildings, and lots of other things from the NPC's of Sunflower-Land. The best way to obtain coins is to sell plants to [Betty](/en/npcs/betty) on your home island.

## Gems

Gems are a ‘Premium’ non-tradeable token that must be purchased from the Game using either Fiat (card/bank) or $FLOWER. Gems are used mostly for restocking your various shops, but can also be used for things like: speeding up cooking/crafting, getting more pet food options, feed animals, and purchase certain ‘craft-ables’ from the blacksmith.

## Chapter Tickets

Chapter tickets are a ‘token’ that can only be collected during its respective [chapter race](/en/chapter-race/basic-info) (3 months each). These tickets can then be used in the [megastore](/en/chapter-race/megastore), or in [auctions](/en/chapter-race/auctions).

At the end of the chapter these tickets become ‘nearly’ worthless. The only place you can ‘use’ them (remove them from inventory) is at [Garbo](/en/npcs/garbo) who will purchase them for a very cheap amount of coins.

## Love Charms

Love charms obtained from MANY different things and can be used on [Mystery Island](/en/events/love-island) for a constantly rotating pool or prizes. (including a pet egg for 10,000 love charms)
