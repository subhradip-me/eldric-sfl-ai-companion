## Marketplace Tax Breakdown

**Resource Taxes (Crops, Wood, Eggs, etc.)**

| Island | Non-VIP | VIP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tutorial (Basic) | Cannot Sell | Cannot Sell |
| Petal Paradise | 55% | 27.5 |
| Desert | 25% | 12.5 |
| Volcano | 15% | 7.5 |

Note: Buying is NOT impacted, this is only for selling.

**Collectible/Wearable taxes are 10%, paid by the seller, regardless of island level or VIP status.**
