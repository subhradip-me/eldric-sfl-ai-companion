### What is Rarecrows?

Rarecrows is an exclusive collectible farming game where players compete to collect unique digital scarecrows while defending their farms from mischievous Goblins and Crows.

Built by Thought Farm, the masterminds behind the legendary Sunflower Land (650,000+ players, 260,000+ holders), Rarecrows brings together strategic farming gameplay with highly sought-after collectibles.

# How to Start?

Rarecrows runs through **Telegram** — you’ll need an account to play.

### 1️⃣ Create a Telegram Account

Download Telegram on mobile or desktop and sign up with your phone number.

### 2️⃣ Join Rarecrows

*   **Recommended:** Start directly in the [Rarecrows Bot](https://t.me/rarecrows)
*   **Alternative:** Play in browser at [beta.rarecrows.io](https://beta.rarecrows.io)

### 3️⃣ Start Farming

Follow the in-game prompts to plant crops, earn coins, and start collecting Rarecrows.

# FAQ

#### **What stage is the Rarecrows mini-game in?**

Rarecrows is currently in **Beta**. Gameplay, drop rates, item stats, and balancing will change rapidly during this period.  
You may notice items being adjusted, temporarily removed, or reworked as we fine-tune the experience.

* * *

#### **Will I keep my Rarecrows and progress after Beta?**

Our intention is **yes** — if everything performs as expected, players will **keep their Rarecrows** when the Beta ends.  
You can also expect to keep any **Upgrades** you’ve earned or applied.

* * *

#### **Is there going to be a token or Jetton?**

Yes! We’re working on integrating a **Jetton on TON** for in-game purchases and player rewards.  
More details about the token launch, utilities, and distribution will be shared soon.

* * *

#### **Will Rarecrows become on-chain collectibles?**

Yes. Rarecrows are designed to become **on-chain digital collectibles**.  
We’re currently experimenting with different technologies and protocols to mint and store these items safely.

Closer to launch, we’ll explain:

*   how to connect your wallet
*   how minting will work
*   what to expect during the migration

Players will be responsible for any **gas fees** and any **risks associated with blockchain transactions** at that time.

_(Note: The specific blockchain is intentionally not announced yet.)_
