# Fermentation & Aging

![](/game-mechanics/aging_shed.png)

Smells delicious….

Welcome to the long game! Salt deposits have been discovered around home islands, and every farmer is racing to rake it up and make use of its properties of preservation. 

## Salt Harvesting

Salt nodes must be unlocked in order to begin harvesting. Salt works a little differently than other minerals. Each node will accumulate one charge every 7 hours, and can hold three charges at once. The number of charges on each node is shown visually (0- empty, 1- sprinkling of salt, 2- mound of salt, 3- pile of salt) and the time for the next one to be added is shown numerically.

To obtain salt, craft a Salt Rake for 3 Wood and 20 Coins. Salt Rakes will be stock limited and require restocks to replenish. No Salt Rakes will be included in the free daily shipment restock. Then, use one Rake per charge on every node, for **10** Salt at the base level. Harvesting does not reset the timer for a node, it will still replenish at the same time whether you harvest with 4 hours or 4 minutes remaining.

Salt is tradable on the marketplace, but there is much more you can do with it.

*   Initial Unlock- 1 Node
    *   30x Wood, 20x Stone, 200x Coins
*   Level 2- 2 Nodes
    *   1000x Coins, 15x Stone, 5x Iron, 100x Salt
*   Level 3- 4 Nodes
    *   4000x Coins, 500x Wood, 40x Gold, 200x Salt
*   Level 4- 6 Nodes
    *   12000x Coins, 100x Gold, 1000x Salt

## Aging Shed

After harvesting some of the sea's salty goodness, you can further unlock its power by building the Aging Shed, at an affordable cost of 200 Coins and 30 Wood. Once it is placed, you will have access to several new features: the Aging Rack, the Fermentation Rack, and the Spice Rack.

Each of these function like their own individual buildings, and have multiple slots where you can run different products simultaneously. Unlike the VIP queue for cooking buildings, all recipes will run concurrently as soon as they are added, and this does not require VIP. You must collect all finished products at the same time. More slots can be obtained by upgrading the building itself, up to 6 per rack. Each upgrade level adds a slot to all racks.

Upgrade Costs- (Number of slots is equal to level)

*   Level 2: 100x Stone, 20x Gold, 3000x Coins
*   Level 3: 500x Wood, 500x Stone, 4000x Coins
*   Level 4: 100x Gold, 10,000x Coins
*   Level 5: 10x Crimstone, 20,000x Coins
*   Level 6: 100,000x Coins

### (Fish) Aging Rack

This process will take fish and age them, which will increase their XP value and allow them to be used in other processes. The amount of Salt and the time needed are directly proportional to the amount of XP that the fish is worth, and can be affected by skills and other factors. Aged Fish (silver) has a chance of being upgraded automatically to a Prime Aged Fish (gold) upon collection. Prime Aged fish are worth 30% more XP when eaten (compared to non-prime aged), and when used to make Fermented Baits, yield double what a normal Aged Fish would provide.

As you can see from the table, the XP/Salt ratio goes down rapidly, with the highest-XP fish requiring huge amounts of salt, not to mention very long processing times. When first starting out, the recommended path to level via Aged Fish is to stick with Anchovy, Red Snapper, or Tuna, as each have a guaranteed bait+chum combination that make them very reliable to harvest in large amounts. Once you start producing fish products, it is better to catch multiple sharks per cast and age those.

All bonuses that affect XP affect Aged Fish, so it is still worth waiting for your Faction Pet bonus to peak at 50% before eating them.

### Fermentation Rack

The most useful job in the building, this allows you to take many resources and create many others. The slot system allows you to process multiple kinds of products at one time.

**Pickled Vegetables**\- Each recipe takes 1 hour. These are only used as ingredients in other recipes, they cannot be eaten directly.

*   Pickled Zucchini- 75x Zucchini + 5x Salt
*   Pickled Tomato- 15x Tomato + 5x Salt
*   Pickled Pepper- 30x Pepper + 5x Salt
*   Pickled Broccoli- 20x Broccoli + 5x Salt
*   Pickled Radish- 10x Radish + 5x Salt
*   Pickled Onion- 10x Onion + 5x Salt

**Greenhouse Fertilizers**\- Each recipe takes 2 hours.

*   Greenhouse Glow- 1x Pickled Tomato, Zucchini, or Broccoli + 1x Refined Salt
    *   Reduces Greenhouse plot growth time by 20%
    *   Like with Rapid Root, this fertilizer will affect the remaining time on a plot, so use it before you plant to get the maximum benefit.
*   Greenhouse Goodie - 1x Pickled Radish, Pepper, or Onion + 1x Refined Salt
    *   Increases Greenhouse plot yield by 0.2

**Combination Fertilizers**\- Each recipe takes 2 minutes.

*   Sproutroot Surprise- 5x Sprout Mix + 5x Rapid Root + 2x Refined Salt
    *   Yields 5 units. Combines the effects of Rapid Root & Sprout Mix (50% growth time and +0.2 yield)
    *   The Knowledge Crab NFT affects this fertilizer also, increasing its bonus to +0.4 yield.
*   Turbofruit Mix- 5x Rapid Root + 5x Fruitful Blend + 2x Refined Salt
    *   Yields 5 units. Reduces fruit patch growth time by 20% and adds 0.1 to yield. The Fruitful Bounty skill **will** increase the yield bonus just like the normal fertilizer.
    *   Like with Rapid Root, applying this fertilizer will affect the **remaining** time before a fruit patch is ready to harvest, so you will want to apply it before you plant a new seed. It will also affect all subsequent harvests, so you can use it right before picking and get the next harvests more quickly.

**Salt Extraction**\- Instant recipe. These can be produced while other recipes are running, even if all slots are full.

*   4x Seaweed for 15x Salt
*   8x Old Bottle for 9x Salt
*   4x Crab for 3x Salt
*   2x Camel Bone for 1x Salt

**Fermented Bait**\- Each recipe takes 5 minutes. Using an Aged Fish (silver) yields 5 units of bait, while a Prime Aged Fish (gold) yields 10. Baits can be used for fishing in place of their listed composter bait, or traded on the Marketplace.

*   Capsule Bait- 1x Pickled Tomato or Zucchini + 1x Basic Aged Fish
    *   Fermented bait that “behaves like an Earthworm”
*   Umbrella Bait- 1x Pickled Broccoli or Pepper + 1x Advanced Aged Fish
    *   Fermented bait that “behaves like a Grub”
*   Crimson Baitfish- 1x Pickled Onion or Radish + 1x Expert Aged Fish
    *   Fermented bait that “behaves like a Red Wiggler”

**IMPORTANT:** All Aged Fish of the same quality are considered equal for purposes of making fermented bait, so there is absolutely no reason to use anything other than an Anchovy, Red Snapper, or Tuna, as they require the least Salt for making in the first place, and will give you the best value for your resources. If you have an Aged Fish of a higher XP value, eat it instead of making it into bait.

### Spice Rack

This process will use Salt and other resources to create some special items.

*   Refined Salt- 10x Salt
    *   Used in other recipes, and to upgrade the Salt Sculpture
*   Salt Lick- 5x Refined Salt
    *   Yields 5 units. Increase Animal produce by 5% over the next 3 harvests
*   Honey Treat- 5x Refined Salt + 5x Honey
    *   Yields 5 units. Reduces Animal feed requirements by 25% over the next 3 harvests

The Salt Lick and Honey Treat are fed to animals like normal feed, except you can use them while they are asleep or awake. You can only apply one or the other to a single animal, not both.

## Salt Skills

Fermentation and aging are so important that they get their own set of skills! Salt nodes are available on all island types except for the Tutorial Island, so these skills will be accessible to most players.

*   Tier 1
    *   Cheap Rakes- Reduces Coin cost of Salt Rakes by 20%
    *   Speedy Aging- Reduces fish aging time by 10%
    *   Salty Seas- Reduces time for Salt nodes to accumulate one charge by 10%
    *   Wide Rakes- +2 Salt per harvest
    *   Bacalhau- +1 Fermented Bait yield (bacalhau is the Portugese word for dried and salted codfish)
*   Tier 2
    *   Fish Smoking- Chance for a Prime Aged Fish increases from 10% to 20%. With a Level 2 Salt Sculpture, the total chance will be 24%, not 28%.
    *   Refiner- 15% chance to get +1 Refined Salt per recipe
    *   Sea Blessed- After harvesting Salt, 5% chance to add 1 charge to up to 4 non-full nodes
*   Tier 3
    *   Ager- All recipes in the Aging Shed take 2x the ingredients and output 2x the products
    *   Salt Surge- Power skill that adds maximum charges to all Salt nodes at once. (72 hour cooldown)

## Salt Sculpture

A new item crafted from the Boosts tab in the landscaping menu, the Salt Sculpture will provide passive boosts related to fermentation and aging. It will be the first upgradeable crafted item, requiring large amounts of resources to fully unlock its power.

It will cost 2000x Coins and 30x Refined Salt to craft initially, which grants the level 1 boost. The boosts are cumulative, meaning a level 6 sculpture will provide every boost listed.

*   Level 1: -5% salt charge replenishment time
    *   Upgrade Cost- 45x Refined Salt, 10x Capsule Bait
*   Level 2: +4% Prime Aging chance
    *   Upgrade Cost- 60x Refined Salt, 10x Capsule Bait, 10x Umbrella Bait, 500x Coins
*   Level 3: +1 max salt harvest cap (4 total)
    *   Upgrade Cost- 70x Refined Salt, 5x Greenhouse Glow, 5x Greenhouse Goodie, 1000x Coins
*   Level 4: -10% Salt Rake coin cost
    *   Upgrade Cost- 85x Refined Salt, 10x Sproutroot Surprise, 10x Turbofruit Mix, 1500x Coins
*   Level 5: “+5% Aging Rack speed” (This boost reduces the time of fish aging recipes by 5%)
    *   Upgrade Cost- 100x Refined Salt, 10x Crimson Baitfish, 2000x Salt, 2000x Coins
*   Level 6: +1 max salt harvest cap (5 total)
