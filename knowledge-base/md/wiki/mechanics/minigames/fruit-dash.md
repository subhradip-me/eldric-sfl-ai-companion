# Fruit Dash

“Survive as long as you can collecting fruits while dodging obstacles in this endless challenge!”

Fruit Dash is one of the minigames in the Kingdom Portal, created by Felga. Completing it gives you a fixed number of Marks, which changes every day along with the goal score. Emblems and items that boost Mark earnings do not affect the payout of this or any other minigame.

## Rules

This is an endless runner game, where your character moves forward constantly, and you can only move them left and right to avoid obstacles. Anything that is not a fruit or powerup will end your attempt immediately, so avoid them as best you can. You have 5 attempts to reach the goal score per day, after which you can pay 1 FLOWER to unlock 3 more attempts, or 3 FLOWER to get unlimited attempts for the remainder of the day.

The speed at which you run increases as the game progresses, making it harder to dodge obstacles, but the value of each fruit also increases the faster you go. The first two powerups you will find will be the Magic Mushroom, which elevates your consciousness to the point that you can pass through obstacles completely, while still picking up fruits as you run. The other is the Golden Pickaxe, which can be thrown at an obstacle to remove it from your path.

After earning 500 points, the Hourglass may appear, which slows the game down by 20%. It will then continue to speed up as normal. You may also find the Pirate Bounty, which is worth a huge amount of points.

You also earn points by passing obstacles, meaning you earn points constantly without needing to collect fruit. You still want to get as many as you can, though!

## Tips/Strategy

*   Use a keyboard! Your precision control with the A/D or left/right keys will be much greater than the tappable buttons on a touchscreen.
*   Don't fret if you miss fruits. More will come, and you are earning points for every obstacle you dodge.
*   If a fruit is right in front of a large obstacle, let it pass by rather than trying to move out of the way at the last second. Unless you have a pickaxe and can clear it out of your way, of course!
*   When you get a mushroom, continue to dodge obstacles to keep your reflexes up for when the effect ends. The mushroom is essentially insurance to make sure you don't hit something. 
*   Prioritize the hourglasses. They are critical to reaching the highest score goals.
*   There is a tab to donate POL to Felga in appreciation of the hard work that went into this game.
