# Home Island

Your home island is where the majority of the game and basic game mechanics happen.

## Mechanics

On your home island there are a number of “main mechanics" that are the “Core” of Sunflower Land's game play loop. (a full list of game mechanic pages can be seen [here](/t/game-mechanics))

*   [Crops](/en/mechanics/harvesting#crops)
*   [Fruits](/en/mechanics/harvesting#fruit)
*   [Mining](/en/mechanics/mining)
*   [Fishing](/en/mechanics/fishing)
*   [Crafting](/e/en/mechanics/crafting)
*   [Skills](/en/mechanics/skills)
*   [Animals](/en/home#animals)

It's important to have a firm grasp of these core mechanics in order to progress yourself in Sunflower Land.

## Buildings

You start out with access to some buildings like; the **House**,  **Firepit**, and **Blacksmith**. But other buildings you will need to build, and others you will need to “prestige your island” to gain access to.

### Basic

*   **water well** (more plot fertility, some plots may not be use-able until you upgrade your water well)
*   **kitchen** (cooking more food types)
*   **crafting box** (access to craft-able items)
*   **compost bin**, **turbo composter**, **premium composter** (access to: fertilizer, fruitful blend, and rapid root)
*   **Bakery** (access to baking cakes)
*   **Deli** (access to more food types, namely Cheese)

### Petal Paradise

*   **hen house** (access to chickens)
*   **barn** (access to cows and sheep)
*   **flowers**
*   **beehives**

### Desert

*   **Crop Machine** (at the cost of oil, grow basic crops automatically; expands to more crops with skills)
*   **Greenhouse** (at the cost of oil, grow Grapes, Rice, and Olives)
*   **Oil**

### Volcano

*   **Lavapits/Obsidian**

## Leveling Up

Leveling up is extremely important, maybe even the MOST important part of Sunflower Land.

Use your cooking buildings to make food from the plants your grow, and the animals you keep. Eat that food by clicking on your bumpkin (in front of your house) and then selecting the food you wish to eat. With each food you eat you gain XP. With enough XP you will level up.

Leveling up will gain you access to more Features and unlock further Expansions for your island.

## Expansions

Expansions are an the only way to make your Island bigger, but they also come with some nice additions to your island. 

When you expand your island you'll gain things like:

*   [Trees/Resource nodes](/en/mechanics/mining)
*   [Crop/Fruit Plots](/en/mechanics/harvesting#farming)
*   [Flower plots](/en/mechanics/flowers#flower-gardening)
*   [Beehives](/en/mechanics/flowers#beehives)
*   [Sunstones](/en/mechanics/sunstone)
*   AND MORE ON BIGGER ISLANDS! (for a full list go [here](https://sfl.world./info/expansion))

* * *

Each island is bigger then the last, and will therefore require more expansions (and more resources) to get to full size.  
Once your island is at its full size, you're ready to Upgrade your island!

## Upgrading Islands (Island Prestige)

There are several Islands that you will prestige through in your Sunflower Land Career. And each comes with special things that you couldn't access on the islands before it.

*   Basic (where you start)
*   Petal Paradise (Flowers, Honey, Animals)
*   Desert (Oil, Greenhouse, Crop Machine)
*   Volcano (Lava pits/Obsidian)
*   Swamp (coming soon)

Upgrading to the Best Island, in most cases, is the only way to ensure you can be “competitive” in the [chapter race](/en/chapter-race/basic-info).

## Conclusion

Initially in Sunflower Land you should: 

1.  Eat food to level up
2.  Expand your Island for more nodes
3.  Prestige to Bigger/Better islands for more features
