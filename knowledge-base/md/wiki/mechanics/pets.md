# Farm Pets

_Not to be confused with the_ [_Faction Pet_](/en/factions/pet)

# Farm Pets

Add a furry (or feathery, or scaly) companion to your farm and enjoy the many benefits of your new friend. Pets are a collectible and progression system designed to create hype, excitement, and a burn mechanic for FLOWER and Chapter progression. They feature an iterative proposal with proof of concept designs.

![444250847-a5516bdc-691a-4589-bfee-5a7474ca1614.gif](/game-mechanics/pets/444250847-a5516bdc-691a-4589-bfee-5a7474ca1614.gif)

Every Pet hatches out of an egg, even those that normally don't in real life! There are several ways for you to get your first egg:

*   Purchase one at the Pet Shop in the Plaza for 150,000 Coins (once ever)
*   Purchase one at the Love Shop on the Floating Island for 10,000 Love Charms (once ever)
*   Complete chores and deliveries to earn one from the Chapter Rewards Pass

Pet Eggs are opened as soon as you purchase or claim them, like Reward Boxes, and the Pet itself will be put in your inventory. Place them somewhere on your farm or in your farmhouse to start feeding and caring for them!

**All of this information is now viewable in the game via the Guide button on a Pet's UI.** Information provided in-game takes precedence over this community-run wiki.

## Pet Types

You can only get one egg from the Pet Shop and Love Shop each, so that will give you two Pets if you can get them both. There are 20 different Pet types, and you will always get a new one every Egg that you buy. So you can't get duplicates! Common pets cannot be traded.

Each Pet has a Category, which determines what kind of items they can fetch for you. There are 20 different Common Pets across 7 categories:

![480440174-8e5f24e6-6b0f-451c-8a8e-b85c0d73dbd9.png](/game-mechanics/pets/480440174-8e5f24e6-6b0f-451c-8a8e-b85c0d73dbd9.png)

*   Guardians fetch Chewed Bones
*   Hunters fetch Ribbons
*   Voyagers fetch Ruffroot
*   Beasts fetch Wild Grass
*   Moonkin fetch Heart Leaf
*   Snowkins fetch Frost Pebble
*   Foragers fetch Dewberry

Every pet has 2 or 3 Categories, so they will be able to get you multiple items. It takes approximately 4.5 years to collect all 20 common pets, provided you can obtain one in each 3-month Chapter.

## Requests

Every day at game reset, your pet will request different foods. When you feed it to them, they will earn Energy and XP. Requests start with 2 per day and increase with pet level. Currently only one unit of each food is required to fulfill the request (unlike the Faction Pet, which usually requires multiples of each food).

New requests are generated every day at game reset, **whether you have completed them or not**. So, make sure you can finish that Fancy Fries before your pet's fancy moves on to something else. You can also reset all the requests with new ones by paying Gems (generates 3 new ones; cost increases with use).

![2025-10-31\_02-48-31.png](/game-mechanics/pets/2025-10-31_02-48-31.png)

The order of the three slots is important; the top slot will be for "Easy" foods, the middle for "Medium" and the lower for "Hard." You can see which foods may appear in a given slot at [https://sfl.world/info/pets](https://sfl.world/info/pets), and plan accordingly. If you have the resources, keeping one of each food on hand will help with whatever requests may come up. Pets will never request anything that requires a fish product or a mushroom as one of its ingredients. It will only ask for things that can be made with resources bought on the Marketplace.

If you can't feed your pet for 3 consecutive days, it will lose 500 XP. This will only happen once, though, so if you need to take an extended leave from the game you will only incur the penalty one time, not every 3 days during your absence. Any of the three slots will fulfill this requirement, it does not need to eat every requested food.

Your pets will sleep periodically, and can be tapped on to gain a small amount of XP (+10 XP every 2 hours during 'nap' mode). In addition, visitors to your farm will pet your creature to give it some bonus XP (+1 to +5 XP, capped at 50 XP per pet per day from social help).

## Fetching

Once your pet has some Energy, you can send it out on quests to find items for you. The Acorn is a standard item that every pet can find, and has multiple different uses. The other items it can find are dependent on its Category, and its level. Sending it to fetch consumes the displayed amount of Energy, and awards the resource instantly, there is no timer or cooldown. The only limit is the amount of energy the Pet has accumulated. Energy does not expire, you can store up as much as you like per pet and have them fetch all at once.

Once your pet reaches a certain level, you will see the amount of resource increase by a decimal amount. This replaces the old mechanic where your pet had a chance to fetch double the resource, which proved to be problematic. This increased amount does not apply to the Fossil Shell, as it is essentially a reward box and not a tradeable resource. The Fossil Shell will always give whole amounts of resources.

Once your pets have gathered some resources, you can use them to craft [Shrines](/en/mechanics/pets#shrines), put them in water traps to bait [Crustaceans](/en/mechanics/fishing#crustaceans), or sell them on the Marketplace to other players who need what their Pets can't fetch yet. Currently the only Pet resource that's used for deliveries are Acorns, but that may change in the future. Acorns can also be used as chum when fishing, though it doesn't seem to differ much from other, easier to get resources.

## Experience

Leveling up your pet will grant it different perks, such as more items available to fetch, more foods available for requests, and other bonuses. There is currently no level cap for a pet, so feed it and watch it grow! XP progression is quadratic (e.g., Level 2: 120 XP, Level 3: 300 XP, Level 4: 600 XP).

## Shrines

Shrines are special buildings that are placed on your farm, and take Pet resources to craft. Resources other than Acorns are tradeable, so you can get them from farmers who have pets of different categories than you. Shrines are crafted in the plaza using the Pet/Shrine Shop and are limited by Acorn input (depends on number of pets fetching Acorns).

Like with all other boosts and items, the effects of a Shrine are only applied when a resource is harvested or processed. For example, the Stag Shrine reduces the Oil well recovery time by 25%. If you currently have an Oil well that has been drilled and is recovering, placing the Shrine will not change the amount of time before the well is ready. For this reason, you should wait until the resources are refreshed and ready (such as first thing in the morning) before placing and activating it. Otherwise, you will spend some of its limited time when it cannot be beneficial to you.

Once a Shrine's timer has expired and its effect has ended, you can choose to remove it from your farm and put it in your inventory, or renew it, paying the resource cost again and restarting the timer. You cannot buy another of the same Shrine at the Pet Shop, so if you want to use it again you must renew the one you already have. All the costs and benefits of the shrines are shown here: [https://sfl.world/info/pets](https://sfl.world/info/pets)

## Pet House

If you keep advancing, you'll find yourself with more than one of these critters running around your farm. To help with their management, you can ask your Blacksmith to construct a Pet House for you. This is a 3x3 structure that can hold 3 Common Pets and 1 NFT pet. It can be upgraded to hold more if you need that.

After placing your pets inside, you can access the house menu with the upper-right icon. From there you can feed all the pets at once, and fetch with only a few taps. You currently cannot place any other items or decorations inside the house, only pets. You also cannot remove the Pet House from your farm without first removing all Pets within it.

There is no bonus or boost given to your pets from being inside, it is purely a convenience feature. This convenience could turn into a drawback, however, as the Pet House is considered a building just like the Barn and Hen House, and therefore it will be disabled if your farm is struck by a Tornado or Tsunami. 

## NFT Pets

Special collectible and tradeable Pets will also be available to those resourceful players who can obtain them. These NFT Pets function just like the commonly-available ones, eating food and taking requests and so on, but have special properties that boost their XP and Energy gains. Their XP and levels will persist even if they are traded to another player, so their value will only grow as you raise them!

NFT Pets are also twice as large as their common counterparts, and take up a 2x2 space on your farm rather than a 1x1 square.

*   **Total Supply**: 3000 NFT Eggs.
*   **Initial Drop**: 1000 Eggs in FLOWER Auction (November 3rd-10th), 50 per auction; players can win multiple (up to 7).
*   **Remaining**: Available each Chapter with tickets (250 total, including Pet Chapter).

### Attributes & Rarity

NFT Pets are one-of-a-kind collectibles with unique characteristics:

*   **Type Chance**: 7 different types. 6 types have a 15% chance each, while the Dragon has a 10% chance.
*   **Fur/Colour Patterns**: 15 unique patterns.
*   **Accessories**: 10 unique accessories (e.g., Wings, Horns).
*   **Bibs**: 3 types, providing XP bonuses on feeds:
    *   33% Chance - Basic (no boost)
    *   33% Chance - Mid (+5 XP)
    *   33% Chance - Great (+10 XP)
*   **Auras**: Multiply energy gains from food. Chances to obtain:
    *   86% - No Aura
    *   8% - Basic Aura (1.5x Energy)
    *   4% - Epic Aura (2x Energy)
    *   2% - Mega Aura (3x Energy)
    *   Total per Chapter: 5 Mega, 10 Epic, 20 Basic.
    *   Initial Drop: 20 Mega, 40 Epic, 80 Basic.

### Exclusive Leveling Perks

NFT Pets have access to unique perks as they level up, enhancing their capabilities beyond common pets:

*   Level 12: Unlock Moonfur Fetching
*   Level 25: Unlock 3rd Fetching Category
*   Level 30: Unlock 4th request (second T2 Food request)
*   Level 40: Pet Experience +15%
*   Level 60: +1 non-acorn/Moonfur resource yield
*   Level 85: Pet Experience +25%
*   Level 150: +0.25 extra Moonfur yield
*   Level 200: Unlock 5th request (2nd T3 Food request)

### Other Benefits

*   NFT Pets can find a unique resource called **Moonfur**, which is highly sought-after.
*   Can be traded on marketplaces; experience is retained on in-game marketplace trades.
*   These Pets will follow their owners around when they visit the Plaza, and other players will be able to pet them directly from the Plaza for bonus XP.

### Limitations

*   Players can manage only 1 pet per type (max 7 NFT pets).
*   Experience retained on in-game trades; lost on withdrawal (removing it from the game to sell on external marketplace).
*   Common pets are not currently tradeable.
*   No in-game naming; suggestions for nicknames.
