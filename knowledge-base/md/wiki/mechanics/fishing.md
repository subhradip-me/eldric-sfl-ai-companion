# Fishing

The oceans are full of aquatic treasures, and fishing is how you'll claim them for yourself! Grab a rod, some bait and chum, and get your feet wet for some satisfying rewards.

Fish are important for a wide range of applications, from cooking and eating, to deliveries, chores, and bounties. Nearly every fish is useful in some way, and you might find some other valuable things at the end of your line…

There are multiple pages for fishing on sfl.world. The landing page is at [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info), while the list of  fish and suggestions on how to catch them are available here: [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/type](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/type)

## Fishing Rods

To get any fishing done, first you need a Rod. Visit the Workbench on your Home Island to make one out of some Wood and Stone, with a small fee of Coins as well. You can decrease the fee by 50% with the skill Reel Deal, but it reduces it from 20 to 10, which will only be a savings in the long run.

Unfortunately the craftsmanship on Rods is rather shoddy, and they will break after a single use. Rods have a stock limit like any other tool, but some are included in the free daily restock.

## Bait

The second component is bait. There are three tiers of bait, corresponding with the three tiers of fish.

*   Basic Bait
    *   Earthworm- Produced from the Compost Bin
    *   Capsule Bait- Made at the [Aging Shed](/en/mechanics/fermentation) from aged basic fish and pickled zucchini/tomatoes.
*   Advanced Bait
    *   Grub- Produced from the Turbo Composter
    *   Umbrella Bait- Made at the Aging Shed from aged advanced fish and pickled broccoli/peppers.
*   Expert Bait
    *   Red Wiggler- Produced from the Premium Composter
    *   Crimson Baitfish- Made at the Aging Shed from aged expert fish and pickled radishes/onions.

Each of the three fermented baits can also be purchased from the Marketplace when you need some more right away.  

You will also get some of the three composter worms from the daily rewards chest, once per week. Bait can be found in some other places, too, so keep exploring!

The Fishing Lure is a special kind of bait that can be obtained by trading with [Finn](/en/npcs/finn) at the Beach, or as a reward from several types of chests. It is used to catch some special fish, so use them sparingly.

## Chum

You can fish with just bait, but you have a much better haul if you add some chum also. Technically chum is optional, and many fish can be caught without it, but most fish have a preferred chum that will increase your chances of catching it substantially. Once you choose a chum, it will remain selected until you change it, so pay attention if you're trying with something rare or valuable. 

## Cast your line

Once you have all of your equipment together, visit the fisher at the bottom-left corner of your Home Island and Cast away! Wait a moment and click or tap on the Reel icon, then see what you've caught.

 There is a limit on the amount of fishing you can do in a day, shown in the window as “# reels left". This can be increased with the skills Fisherman's 5 Fold (+5), Fisherman's 10 Fold (+10), and More with Less (+15). There are also many items and decorations which increase fishing limit, some of which you'll earn just through fishing. If you find yourself completely out of reels, you can buy 5 more for 5 Gems.

The fish you get is determined by a random roll, influenced by your bait, chum, and the current season. There are some fish that can be caught 100% of the time, regardless of season, with the right combination.

*   Anchovy
    *   Basic Bait + 10x Carrot (cheapest)
    *   Basic Bait + 5x Egg
*   Tuna
    *   Advanced Bait + 3x Orange
    *   Advanced Bait + 1x Wild Mushroom (technically cheapest, since mushrooms are free, but can't be bought in bulk like Oranges)
*   Red Snapper
    *   Expert Bait + 3x Apple (cheapest)
    *   Expert Bait + 1x Honey

Since these three are the most commonly requested fish for deliveries and are used in the fish market and [aging shed](/en/mechanics/fermentation), they are worth memorizing, especially during the Fish Frenzy.

## Multi cast

If you have VIP status, you can work your fisherperson extra hard and spend multiple rods and reels at the same time. Just select the number you want to use at once, ensure you have all the resources you need, and cast a wide net. You will use the same bait and chum for every cast, and unless you choose one of the guaranteed catch combinations, you will get a wide variety of fish in return. All bonuses and buffs will resolve automatically.

If you do choose to use a fish product for a guaranteed catch, you must choose the same fish for every cast.

## Fish Types

Fish are categorized into types, just like many other things in the game. You can see each of the basic, advanced, and expert fish in your Codex, along with which you've caught, the season to find them in, and some suggestions on how to catch them again. There are several skills (the Fishy group) that will increase the chances of you getting another fish when you catch one in a specific category.

Marine Marvels are a rare type of fish that require special circumstances, but are so valuable that you can place them on your farm in a tank for all to see.

Chapter Fish are even rarer Marvels that are only available during the current Chapter and provide different boosts. But, you can trade some of them on the marketplace if you missed out.

The Codex also shows you the rewards you can earn from certain fishing achievements. There's no indication in the game as to when you unlock one, so check back often!

## Cooking & Preparing (Fish Market)

Fish can be eaten for XP, but it is almost never a good idea to do so. Foods prepared from fish are **always** worth more XP, and some are even used for deliveries. The only time you would want to eat fish raw is when a Weekly Chore requests you to do so. Even then, consider whether the tickets and points are worth the value of the fish in products or recipes.

The skill Fishy Feast increases the XP of raw fish, aged fish, **and** foods made with fish products. All other boosts that increase the XP value “of fish” affect eating fish directly and foods prepared with them.

You'll notice that your cooking buildings don't take raw fish as ingredients. First you must prepare your catches at the Fish Market, another building that you need to place on your farm. There, you can produce four different items with the fish and crustaceans you have caught, and then cook them at your Kitchen and Firepit. It may take a while to prepare the fish product, but the edible recipes are cooked instantly, to save you time! What's more, if you are a VIP player you can cook an instant recipe right away, even if all of your queue slots are full.

The Fish Market is not considered a cooking building, so boosts that affect cooking time or output do not apply to fish preparation. There are other NFT items that can boost your fish processing times, though.

There are only four fish products, but the ingredients that go into them change every in-game Season. As always, you can see the complete schedule at [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#FISH\_MARKET\_RECIPES](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#FISH_MARKET_RECIPES)

## Guaranteed Catch

Products prepared at the Fish Market are not only tasty for your Bumpkin, they are irresistible to fish too. Sometimes you just can't afford to spend all your rods and reels on pot luck, and need a specific fish for a delivery or recipe. Fortunately, you can use the power of fish products to ensure that you always get the fish you need instead of a lot of Seaweed (unless you want Seaweed, of course).

Choose a fish product as your Bait when fishing. Then, you can pick exactly which fish you want to pluck out of the ocean. You can't use Chum when doing a guaranteed catch. 

The complete list of guaranteed catches with fish products is at [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#FISH\_MARKET\_GUARANTEED](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#FISH_MARKET_GUARANTEED)

You may notice that there is no mention of Season in the guaranteed list. This is because with products, you can catch **any** fish during **any** Season. This will help make your fishing empire grow much more smoothly, and allow you to make deliveries more easily and get the category rewards much more easily.

You can use VIP multi-cast with products, but you will get the same fish on every cast.

## Crustaceans

While fish are definitely valuable, there are other aquatic creatures that just can't be caught with a rod and reel. Next to your pier will be several posts in the water, some of which will have crabs at the bottom, others will have a gull on top. These are where you can place your water traps. You will gain more automatically as you upgrade your island.

Visit your Workbench on your farm to have the Blacksmith put together some Crab Pots or Mariner Pots. Both have their own stock that is replenished with the paid Workbench Restock, not the free daily Restock. Once you have at least one, set it out on a pier and wait. Crab Pots take 4 hours to finish, and Mariner Pots take 8. Make sure to select a chum to go with it, though.

If you have at least one of the resources that can be used as a chum, it will appear in the window, but you may not have enough of that resource to actually use it. As always, you can see the complete list of chums and what they catch here [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#CRUSTACEANS](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#CRUSTACEANS)

Another major difference from fishing is that each chum will **always** catch the corresponding crustacean. There is no RNG involved. Using no chum in a Crab Pot will always get you an Isopod, never any other kind of creature.

Bonuses that affect the number of fish caught do not work for water traps, including the Fishing Frenzy event. There are other NFT items that will boost your pot readiness time and the yield from each one.

Crustaceans are used to complete requests on the Mega Bounty Board, and for deliveries to a certain NPC. You can also use them to create a Crab Stick at your Fish Market, which is then used to catch some very valuable fish. You can also cook the Crab Stick into an edible meal on its own.

## Treasures

There are other things you can catch other than fish, such as Seaweed, Sea Cucumbers, and Old Bottles. These are valuable on their own, but do not count as fish for the purposes of boosts that increase the number of fish you get per catch. Some are used for deliveries and crafting, others can be sold at [Jafar's shop](/en/mechanics/digging) at the Beach for Coins, and a few can be processed into Salt at the [Aging Shed](/en/mechanics/fermentation).

## Marine Marvel Maps

Marine Marvels are rare fish that take some work to find and catch, and are so valuable that you can place them as decorations on your farm! There are five permanent Marvels that can be caught at any time, and between 1-3 Chapter Marvels that are only obtainable during the current Chapter, but can usually be bought from the Marketplace later on.

Finding one isn't just up to a random number roll like it used to be, either. When catching certain fish, you may see a sparkle in the water and get an opportunity to score a Map Piece for a specific Marvel. Complete the minigame and it will be yours!

### Marvel Minigame

You will be shown a series of squares and need to click on one per row, starting at the bottom. If you find the correct path (arrow), you can then click on the next higher row. If you click on a crab, you will briefly be shown a few steps ahead and then all squares will flip back over. Remember the path as you uncover it and you'll be at the top in no time. If you choose incorrectly too many times, don't worry, just pay 1000 coins and get 4 more attempts. The Anemone Flower obtainable on the marketplace will give you an extra chance when map-finding.

Once you complete the minigame, you will get a map piece corresponding to one Marvel. Once you get all 9, the Marvel is yours! You can see how many map pieces you've discovered on the Fishing tab of your Codex.

### Map Piece Chances

Only certain fish have a chance of dropping map pieces, and some more than others. The table below lists the five “permanent” marvels, that are obtainable at any time regardless of in-game season or chapter. For the current chapter marvels, check out [https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#MAP\_PIECES](https://sfl.world/info/fishing/info#MAP_PIECES) 

If you catch multiple fish using VIP multi cast, you can obtain multiple map pieces as well.

The Deep Sea Helm increases the chances of a piece dropping by +100%. Note this does not make a map piece guaranteed, it just adds the base chance to the total (i.e. if the base chance to get a map piece is 5%, the DSH increases the chance to 10%). This helmet is obtainable by catching at least 5 of every Basic, Advanced, and Expert Fish. Using fish products for a guaranteed catch on a Fish Frenzy day is an excellent way to accomplish this quickly.

The Full Moon monthly Event doubles the chances of a piece dropping again (+100%). For some Marvels this makes the chance over 100%, guaranteeing you will get a piece if you catch the required fish. For others, it makes a miniscule chance slightly less so.

The seasonal Guardians obtainable through the marketplace or at auction also affect the Full Moon Event, giving you a +200% increase for the day.

The Navigation Table item introduced in the Salt Awakening Chapter increases the chances by yet another +100%.

## Fish Frenzy

Every so often, the waters around your island will teem with life and a Fish Frenzy will begin. You will earn an additional fish every time you make a successful catch. This is on top of any other boosts you have that increase fish quantity, such as the Fishy skills. If those skills trigger you will get 3 fish, with the possibility for more.

The skill Frenzied Fish is considered the ultimate during the Fish Frenzy, as the event bonus increases to +2, and you have a 50% chance to get +3. Once again, every other chance for an additional fish still applies. 

The seasonal Guardian items increase the bonus of a Fish Frenzy to +2 when it occurs in their season.

## History

With the addition of the Fish Market and prepared products, many fish recipes were removed from the various cooking stations. All cooking recipes now use prepared fish products instead of raw fish. This caused some confusion during the first week of the new Chapter, when the Faction Pet for some factions didn't get the news and still asked for foods that couldn't be cooked any longer! Fortunately the issue was fixed quickly, and the Pets now content themselves with the new foods instead.

Previously there was another fisher NPC at the Beach, near the entrance to the Plaza. Fishing from there was functionally the same as fishing from your home island, but it meant there were two separate chance tables for each fishing location: Ocean fishing from the Beach, and Wharf fishing from the home island. Ultimately this was determined to be too complicated, so Corale chased off the Beach fisher and he hasn't been seen since.

In Chapters past, there was a time of day mechanic that affected fishing. Dawntide and Dusktide were the two halves of the day, and the chances for certain fish changed along with those tides. This was removed with Seasons, which now govern which fish can be caught and when.
