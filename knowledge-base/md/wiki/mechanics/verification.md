# How to Complete In-Game Verification via Discord or Telegram

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for verifying your identity in-game using your linked Discord or Telegram account.

* * *

## Discord Verification

Follow the steps below to complete verification through Discord:

### Step 1 – Agree to Terms

*   Go to the **⁠╭📙・rules** channel in the server.
*   Read and agree to the game's **Terms and Conditions**.

### Step 2 – Request Verification

*   Navigate to the **⁠｜🔓・verify** channel.
*   Type `/verify` exactly, including the slash (`/`).
*   Submit the command.

### Step 3 – Complete In-Game Verification

*   Return to the game on your device.
*   Follow the prompts to complete the **face verification** step.

### Platform-Specific Notes

#### On Mobile:

1.  Tap the **game icon** in Discord.
2.  Select **Flower Rewards Bot**.
3.  Press the **Send button** next to the **Verify** option.

#### On Desktop:

1.  Click the **Plus (+)** icon in the message box.
2.  Select **Use Apps** (or **Apps**).
3.  Choose the **`/verify`** command.
4.  Press **Enter** to send.

## Telegram Verification

### Step 1 – Open the Bot

*   Go to: [https://t.me/pumpkin\_pete\_bot](https://t.me/pumpkin_pete_bot)
*   Start the bot if you haven’t already.

### Step 2 – Send Verification Command

*   In the chat, type: **`/verify`** (including the slash).
*   Send the message.

### Step 3 – Finalize In-Game

*   Switch back to the game.
*   Proceed with **face verification** as prompted.

* * *

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues during verification, the most common reason is:

> You are using a **Discord or Telegram account that is NOT linked** to the game account you’re trying to verify.

### How to fix:

*   Log out of Discord/Telegram on your device.
*   Log in with the **correct account** that you used when registering for the game.
*   Repeat the verification steps above.

If the problem persists, contact the game’s support team through official channels for further assistance.
