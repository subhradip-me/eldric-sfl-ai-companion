# Chickens

Thinking about raising chickens? Here's a guide to help you understand the mechanics and benefits you can gain.

## Hen House

To start raising chickens, you first need to build the Hen House. You can build this in Blacksmith when you reach level 6. The base building allows you to raise/sell 10 chickens, upgrade 1 allows you to raise/sell 15 chickens, and upgrade 2 allows you to raise/sell 20 chickens.

> Chicken Coop NFT gives you +5 chickens for each building level

Costs:

*   100 Coins
*   30 Wood
*   5 Iron
*   5 Gold

## Buy/Sell chickens

Buy the chickens for 50 coins.

Sell your chickens every week to earn coins, season tickets, or gems. Once you complete a sale, you'll have to wait for the weekly reset to see different bounties. The amounts vary depending on the chicken's level. You can deliver a higher-level chicken even if the customer requests a lower level chicken.

> When an animal becomes sick, it will drop half the amount of resources & sell for 25% less at the exchange

Hen House Level 1

*   8 Season tickets
*   2 Coins

## Feeder machine

Prepare food for your chickens using the Feeder machine, Animals prefer their favourite food but will eat any food. Omnifeed acts as the best feed for all the animals.  
The check for sickness happen once every 24hrs and there is the chance of it spreading (sickness chance increases for every sick animal you have). You can heal the chickens with the Barn delight.

###### Food

Kernel Blend

*   1 corn

Hay

*   1 wheat

NutriBarley

*   1 Barley

Mixed Grain

*   1 Corn
*   1 Wheat
*   1 Barley

Omnifeed

*   1 Gem

###### Medicine

Barn Delight

*   5 Lemons
*   3 Honey

## Love tools

You can craft three tools to gain XP on chickens twice while they sleep:

*   Petting Hand(Free): +25 XP bonus
*   Brush(2000 coins): +40 XP bonus
*   Music Box(50000 coins): +50 XP bonus
