# Infernos/Sunstones

![](/game-mechanics/infernos-sunstones/map.jpg)

## Infernos

Infernos can be accessed from the map, using the globe icon in the bottom left of the screen. You must be level 30 to travel there.

Infernos is the home of 3 Infernal Goblins: [Gunter](/en/npcs/gunter), [Gilda](/en/npcs/gilda), and [Gorga](/en/npcs/gorga). It is also the place where you can exchanges Obsidian for Sunstones, and Sunstones for more ‘nodes’ or ‘node forging’.

## Sunstones

Sunstones are a ‘Finite’ resource that, unlike the other resources, does NOT refresh once fully mined. When you mine a Sunstone node 10 times, it disappears from your island. Sunstones require a Gold Pickaxe to mine. If you're curious about how much Sunstone is remaining in your nodes, you can check [https://sfl.world/land/](https://sfl.world/land/) (enter your farm ID). Each individual node displays its remaining value visually, getting smaller and smaller as you continue to mine it.

You will gain new Sunstone rocks as you expand your island, though, so eventually more will come.

If you just can't wait, though, visit **Gorga** on Infernos island, who will trade you 1 Sunstone for 3 Obsidian. Obsidian is obtained from the Lava Pits available after you upgrade to the Volcano island.

![](/game-mechanics/infernos-sunstones/exchange-sunstone.jpg)

## Node Purchase and Forging

By visiting **Gunter** at the left side of the island you can exchange your Sunstones for various resource nodes to add to your home island. The price of each node increases as you craft them, and as always the amounts are listed at [https://sfl.world/info/const/#RESOURCE\_NODE\_PRICES](https://sfl.world/info/const/#RESOURCE_NODE_PRICES)

![](/game-mechanics/infernos-sunstones/buy-nodes.jpg)

The Upgrade tab in Gunter's window allows you to combine, forge, or fuse resource nodes together, which will increase your resource output tremendously. Trees, Stones, Iron, and Gold rocks can all go through this process, with two tiers per node type. The cost to do this may be high, but the benefits will soon outweigh the price.

Each Tier 2 node takes up the same amount of space as the standard node, but contains 4 nodes worth of resource, that is, you will extract four times more resources from the node per harvest. You do, however, need 4 tools to actually harvest it (4 Axes, Pickaxes, etc).

Tier 3 nodes require 4 Tier 2 nodes to forge, which results in a total of 16 tools worth of resource in one harvest!

In addition to saving space, each higher-tier node provides an **additional** boost to resource output. Every node you want to use in this process must be ready/fresh, that is, it must not have a cooldown timer after having been harvested. Remove them from your farm before visiting Infernos to make sure you only use the ones you want.

With the new [Ascension](/en/mechanics/ascension) system, eventually you will gain more resource nodes than you have space for on your farm. Don't forget that you can combine them and make space for more!
