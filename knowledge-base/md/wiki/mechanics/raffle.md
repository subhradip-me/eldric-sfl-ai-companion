**Heads up! The Goblin Raffle has been discontinued and merged with the** [**weekly chapter raffles**](/en/chapter-race/raffles)**. This article now serves as a list of ways to obtain Prize Tickets.**

# Goblin Raffle

The monthly raffle was a special event where you could win big rewards. 5 players won 1000x FLOWER, while 2 players got a Bud NFT. 

The Goblin Raffle winners were announced on the Friday Twitch Streams in the 1st or 2nd Fridays of the month.

  
Getting a ticket, however, was a feat to itself. Despite the game telling you that you can find a Prize Ticket by doing NPC deliveries, no character will actually give you one just by earning points. What they **will** give you is a treasure chest key, which is the primary source of tickets at this time.

You can see the probability of getting one for each chest type at [https://sfl.world/info/chests](https://sfl.world/info/chests). If you have a Bud already, you could potentially earn one through the Bud Chest.

If you are a master digger, you have a fairly good chance of getting a Prize Ticket if you can keep up your streak for 11 or more days. The chances of that are shown at the above link also.

A Prize Ticket is also one of the available items at the Marketplace Rewards Shop. It's the most expensive thing there, but you can also buy treasure keys, so if you're tired of eating Trade Cakes you can try spending your rewards on those instead. 

In the event you get your hands on one of these coveted items, visit the Prizes chest in the Plaza and see when the next raffle will be that accepts your hard-won Ticket!
