# P2P Marketplace

The in-game marketplace is the very heart of Sunflower Land, as it allows players from all over the world to trade resources with one another and ultimately help everyone succeed in their game. With so much to do and see, it can be overwhelming to try and do everything at once. Fortunately, you don't have to grow it yourself, you can buy it! Give some food to those hungry goblins and get a few FLOWER, or deposit some from outside the game, and let's get trading.

**This article assumes you have reached the Cropkeeper** [**reputation level**](/en/mechanics/reputation) **and unlocked full access to the Marketplace. Otherwise, you may have restrictions or limits in place.**

## Overview

The marketplace is easy to access, just tap the icon with left and right arrows in the lower-left corner of your screen. There are lots of buttons and links, so lets go through each one.

### Trending

The first tab, shown in the screenshot, displays some stats about recent activity and high-value items sold in the last few days. From there you can search or browse the listings directly.

### Resources

This is the directory of everything you can grow, mine, and ranch on your farm. An individual listing contains the item's price and how many you have.

### Power ups

A directory of every item, whether wearable or decorative, that will provide you with a boost of some kind in the game. You can narrow it down to one of those two categories with the links that appear in the left-hand menu.

### Cosmetics

Everything you can wear or place on your farm that doesn't have a boost of any kind. These are just for fun and style, and are usually much cheaper than boost items. But some rare ones can still be worth quite a bit…

### Pet NFTs

If the common pets are just too boring for you, visit this section to find a unique creature to call your own!

### Bud NFTs

Visit this page if you've got resources to invest in your game journey, or just want to look at their adorable little faces! Buds are a rare item that are worth several thousand FLOWER at the least, but can provide some major boosts to your gameplay.

### My Profile

Some stats about the last few things you've bought, and your active trades. You can also see your whole farm's inventory and, if possible, sell something you don't need. There are many non-tradeable items listed here, like your house and firepit.

### Reward Shop

Under My Profile, this is where you'll spend all of those Trade Points you'll eventually earn.

### Flower Dashboard

Look here for some fun stats and numbers about the economy.

## Making a Deal

Once you find something that strikes your fancy, you will see several options on the screen. If you're looking to Buy something, find a row under Listings that fits your budget and click the button to trade. You'll get the items instantly and pay the FLOWER.

### Bulk Buy

What if you need a LOT of something, or want to pick up several smaller listings to get everything you need? Just use the Bulk Buy button, which will let you select multiple listings and purchase them all at once. You can also specify the amount of resource you need and the game will automatically select listings that come as close as possible to your desired number without going over. You'll be able to verify which ones you want before purchasing, too.

### Offers

But what if you don't want to buy 50 Leather, especially at those prices? What if you only need 5 to craft that doll? Then scroll down to the bottom of the Offers section that isn't visible in the screenshot and click Make Offer. Enter in how many you need, and the game will tell you if you go under the minimum that every item has. The total price for the sale is prefilled to the right, but you can click the Price button to change it to Price Per Unit, if that way of thinking makes more sense to you. You can leave the price as it is, but to make your offer more enticing to sellers, you should generally price it slightly **higher** so it appears at the top of the Offers list. But it's really up to you.

After you make an offer, you will have to wait for someone to accept it and sell to you. It may take a while, but if you priced it right, it should be fairly quick. You'll get a popup when you first load the game, or if you try to do other actions. Congratulations on your successful trade!

### Listings

After your latest bountiful harvest, you're looking to sell some of that Kale. You have two options when selling something, either accepting someone else's Offer, or making a new Listing. First scroll through the Offers list and see if any look good. Offers are usually always lower than the official price at the top of the screen, but the advantage is that you will get your money instantly, instead of waiting for a Listing.

Use the List for Sale button at the top to make that happen. The Listing screen looks almost the same as the Offers screen, except for the trading fee. The marketplace taxes every transaction, with the fees paid by the seller. When you made your offer earlier, you weren't selling, so you didn't pay the fee. You can see your current resource fee on the right side of the screen, and like it suggests, advancing in the game will lower this fee substantially.

Draw up your Listing with quantities and price, then submit it and wait for a buyer. When selling, you want your prices to be **lower** than the other farmers, so even just reducing it by 0.0001 FLOWER is enough to put you at the top of the queue. Or, hold your position and hope for a better deal. Again, it's up to you.

Quantity is also important for Listings, since if you sell 500 Kale at once, it might take a while for someone with that kind of budget to come along. It's generally better to split up a large amount of a resource across several Listings with smaller quantities. This is very easy to do, just change the # field at the right of the screen to the number of listings you want to create simultaneously, all for the same quantity. You can create up to 20 in one go with this method.

Like with Offers, Listings depend on another player to transact, so you might have to wait a while, especially if the demand for a resource or item is low. If your Kale isn't moving, cancel the Listing and try for a different price until you get it.

### Obsidian

Obsidian is a rare and powerful resource, and used to be available on the Limited tab of the marketplace before it was removed. Despite being shown in the standard Resources list now, it still retains some of its old limitations. **Currently, each player can only list 1 piece of Obsidian for sale per week, and buy a total of 5.** Despite Obsidian being divisible into decimal units through instantly growing flowers and giant fruits, you can only buy and sell a single unit at a time. This means if you want more than one you must buy each individually.  
You also earn Trade Points when buying or selling Obsidian.

### Item Trading

The process to trade NFT collectibles or wearables is slightly different than it is for resources.  
You can only list one copy of an item for sale at a time, even if you have more than one.  
The tax for trading items is fixed at 10%, regardless of your progression in the game.  
When buying a collectible item, you don't get to choose which listing you want to purchase. The Buy Now button will let you buy the first item in the list, which is the lowest-priced.  
If you're selling and choose to Accept an offer, you will sell to the first player in the list, which will be the highest-priced.

This requires a little more strategy than when selling resources, where your listings can be bought up even if someone else creates a listing at a lower price.  
If you want to make sure your item is chosen by a buyer, you have to watch it periodically and re-list it if someone undercuts you.  
Or, if you want to make sure your offer is accepted by a seller, bid a little more than the next farmer.

## Searching

If you're looking for something to help a specific aspect of your game, you can search for the kind of boost you want in addition to the name of the item. Searching for “tomato” not only gives you the Tomato Clown and the Tomato Bombard, but also Cannonball, all of which boost your Tomato plants in some way.

## Reward Shop

After you've bought or sold some NFT items, you may notice a few things in your inventory called Trade Points. These are earned at a rate of 0.25 for each 1 FLOWER of the item's list price (pre-tax).

Visit the Rewards tab under My Profile to see what you can redeem all those points for. [Treasure Keys](/en/mechanics/chests) are a good investment, or you can try your luck in the Bud Raffle with the rare [Prize Ticket](/en/chapter-race/raffles). The Trade Cake is worth a decent amount of XP, if you don't feel like gambling.

There are also several valuable packs available. These items are given to you directly, not added to your shops [stock amounts](/en/beginners/stock-limits), as they seem to indicate.

The Tools Pack claims to have a full Stock's worth of tools, which it would if you didn't have any other items or skills that boost your tool stock amounts. It also only contains pickaxes of every variety and regular Axes.

The [Digging](/en/mechanics/digging) Pack contains 50 Sand Shovels and 10 Drills, naturally.

The [Seeds](/en/mechanics/harvesting) Pack contains _most_ of a un-boosted stock's worth of seeds, except it is missing several. All of the single-season crops added in the Winds of Change Chapter are absent, as are flower and greenhouse seeds. It will warn you if you have so many seeds that you will go over your inventory limit with at least one of them, but given its limited nature this isn't really worth your hard-earned TP.

The [Fishing](/en/mechanics/fishing) Pack contains those missing rods from the Tools Pack, along with a good assortment of baits. If you don't have the higher level composters, this is a decent choice.

The [Animal](/en/mechanics/animals) Feed Pack contains the four major food groups for any of your animals, except Omnifeed. Given the price of Mixed Grains, this is worth considering if you are an animal expert.

# External Marketplaces

It is still possible to buy and sell resources and items on other NFT marketplaces such as [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/collection/sunflower-land-collectibles). However, as the in-game marketplace has evolved, other marketplaces have become less relevant, as they are less accessible to new players unfamiliar with blockchain concepts.

You must pay for your resource or item in crypto, and transfer it from your wallet to your game account, every step of which requires a gas fee and the POL token. The game used to provide a Polygon wallet address tied to your farm, which allowed you to skip a step and have the marketplace send the NFT directly to the farm. That has been removed, so you must receive it in your personal wallet.

Resource withdrawal has been removed from the game, so any Sunflowers that you find for sale somewhere else were extracted before the change. It is only possible to Deposit resources that you may still have in your wallet.

All other items are still withdrawable and tradeable, including powerups, cosmetics, Buds, and NFT Pets.

# History

The marketplace used to be only accessible from the Plaza, at a large notice board that stood where [Bailey](/en/npcs/bailey) currently has his shop set up. You had to travel to the Plaza to be able to create listings or make offers, and your goods or money would be delivered to you wherever you were as it happens today. The button in the UI that lets you access it anywhere was added before Bailey set up shop, so you could still get to it from the Plaza or the button.

### Limited Section

A rotating list of items that were requested on the [Mega Bounty Board](/en/chapter-race/bounty-board) for the week. The Limited tab was removed at the beginning of Chapter 13. For every item on this page, you could buy 3 of them and sell 1 in a week, excepting Obsidian, which you could buy 9 of. Even at Cropkeeper with “unlimited access” this restriction was still in place.

### Resource Withdrawal

Previously, resources could be withdrawn from a player's account and stored as SFTs in their crypto wallet, then sold on external marketplaces. To make the game less attractive to players who only wanted to make money, this option was removed in March of 2026. Only certain resources were withdrawable, and even then the game charged a 30% tax when doing so.
