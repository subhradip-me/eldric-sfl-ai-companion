# Reputation

## Introduction

Reputation is a system that was implemented by the devs to try and combat “bad actors”, “multi accounters” and “bots”. The system aims itself to allow only REAL people to play the game and benefit from certain features that the game has to offer. This system has been successful overall, as bot activity which used to be rampant has largely subsided.

## How to get reputation points?

Reputation points can be gained a number of ways

*   Reach Petal Paradise - 100 points
*   Reach lvl 15 - 50 points
*   Reach Desert Island - 50 points
*   Reach Volcano Island - 50 points
*   Connect & verify Discord - 50 points
*   Complete proof of Humanity - 100 points
*   Reach lvl 100 - 150 points
*   Own a bud - 300 points
*   VIP Access - 600 points (If your VIP status lapses you will lose the points)

## What do I gain by having reputation points?

At certain “milestones” of rep points you will gain the ability to do more things in the game.

###   
Seedling - 250 points

*   2 Marketplace Listings Per Day
*   Withdraw NFTs (decorations and wearables)
*   Ability to Mint Your Farm (This provides you with an NFT token that represents access to your farm. It also allows you to “Store on Chain," a process required to exceed the storage limits for some items)

### Grower - 350 points

*   3 Marketplace Listings Per Day
*   Auction Access

### Cropkeeper - 600 Points

*   Make & Accept Offers
*   Unlimited Listings & Offers
*   Unlock all $FLOWER Deliveries

### Grand Harvester- 1000 Points

*   Withdraw FLOWER

## How can I find how many reputation points I have?

1.  Click your profile Icon at the top left corner of the screen. (image 1)
2.  Click the “reputation button" (image 2)
3.  Click “Earn Points” in the top tab to view your points, and what you still need to do to get more (image 3)
