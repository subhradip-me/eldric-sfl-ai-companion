# Farmhands

As you explore more of the game, you may find yourself with multiple pieces of wearable equipment that provide useful boosts, but only one slot to put them in. While you can wear two shirts in real life, your Bumpkin can't, so you'll have to invite some friends to help you maximize your potential!

## Farmhand Usage

Farmhands are extra characters that you can assign to your farm and have them wear additional pieces of equipment. If you have two hats that both provide a benefit, you can't equip them both to your primary Bumpkin. With an extra Farmhand, you can use them, and even more. You can have as many Farmhands as there are Beds available in the game. Enter your Farmhouse and tap on the Add Farm Hand button to see the current lineup. More are added frequently, so check back each Chapter.

Farmhands come with their own random (cosmetic-only) equipment when hired, so don't worry if you don't have enough shirts and pants to clothe them all.

If you decide you really like the style of one of your Farmhands and want to show it off in the plaza, tap the Set as Bumpkin button. This will set your active character to be the Farmhand instead, meaning you control them when making deliveries and visiting areas outside your farm. If you change your mind, just find your last character and promote them back again.

The primary Bumpkin is the one you feed to gain XP, so either tap on them wherever they are on your farm, or tap the portrait button in the upper-left and select Feed Bumpkin.

## Beds

Bumpkins won't stick around your farm without a place to sleep, so you'll have to build them a bed if you want them to help out. If you started playing the game recently, you should already have a Basic Bed in your house. If you don't, you will need to make one before you can build the others. Get a Crafting Box if you haven't already, and follow the recipes to build a Basic Bed. Place it in your farm, then your primary Bumpkin will claim it. Then, start work on the next one. The recipes for all of the beds can be found at [https://sfl.world/info/crafting](https://sfl.world/info/crafting)

You do not have to build them in any specific order, but the order shown in the Farmhouse menu is the recommended one, due to the price and complexity of the components needed for each one.

Beds are also rewarded through participation in the current Chapter Race, so keep an eye out for where they might be available every few months.

## Placement on the Farm

Farmhands may get tired of just hanging out on the porch of your house, so let them roam about! Farmhands can be picked up and moved anywhere on your farm just like a decoration or resource node. If you remove them, they will return to standing in front of your farmhouse and not go into the Chest.

Currently, the location of a Farmhand doesn't affect the boosts provided by their equipment.
