# Ascension

**This feature is new as of Chapter 15, and may change at any time. Please refer to the game for the most accurate info.**

Source**:** [https://github.com/sunflower-land/sunflower-land/discussions/7343](https://github.com/sunflower-land/sunflower-land/discussions/7343) 

Volcano Island is not the end! After expanding your island as far as it will go, you will be prompted by a helpful Goblin to Upgrade your island to a new type (Basic→Spring→Desert→Volcano). Now you can keep on going, and gain some powerful enhancements along the way!

Upon reaching Bumpkin Level 150 and expanding the Volcano Island to its maximum, you will be presented with the option to Ascend. This will have several immediate effects, including lowering your level back to 50, raising your Ascension Level (A) and changing your island to a new type. Despite your level being lowered, your total skill points will not go down, in fact it will continue to go up as you keep leveling. This will let you unlock even more skills and bonuses, and use the Ascension Shard items you find to push your boosts even further.

## **Basic Ascension Flow**

*   Reach Level 150 and max out Volcano Island
*   Upgrade to reach Swamp Island (A1)
*   Expand Swamp Island 12 times and earn 50 XP levels
*   Upgrade to Spooky Island (A2)
*   Repeat (50 levels, 12 expansions):
    *   Crystal Island (A3)
    *   Galaxy Island (A4)
    *   Marble Island (A5+)

You will continue to earn skill points for each of the 50 levels needed to reach the next Ascension, and you will continue to gain new resource nodes, crop plots, and all the things you usually find when expanding your island. This makes [node forging](/en/mechanics/sunstone) more useful than ever.

New biomes are available in the Landscaping Shop for each of the island types if you decide you really like the look of one and don't want to leave it behind as you continue your rise to glory.

## Ascension Shards

Gained from Ascension Crystals, these special nodes will appear on your island after certain expansion levels. When mined with a Gold Pickaxe, they give you 3 Ascension Shards, then disappear completely, with no replenishment. Shards are used to upgrade learned [skills](/en/mechanics/skills) to higher tiers. Higher-tier skills have greater bonuses, but also more severe penalties…

After moving from the Basic (Tutorial) island to Petal Paradise, you will find one Ascension Crystal node as you expand your land. After upgrading to Desert Island, you will uncover another one in the sands, and a third one is buried under the ashes of the Volcano Island.

After Ascending beyond Volcano Island, you will get more Crystals as you continue to expand, so don't worry. It is totally possible to unlock every rank of every skill! 

If you were an existing player before the Ascension Age Chapter started, you will get some Ascension Crystal nodes as soon as you complete your next expansion, and will gain more as you continue to expand your island.
