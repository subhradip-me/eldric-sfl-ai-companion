# Chests

## Reward Chests

There are several different treasure chests that can be opened through various means in the game. The possible rewards from every chest are listed at [https://sfl.world/info/chests](https://sfl.world/info/chests)

The contents of the Keyed Chests change every Chapter, while the others do not change very frequently. As with all games of chance, it's important to have an open mindset when opening any chest. Even if you don't get the item you wanted, whatever you receive will likely be useful in some way, even if later.

### Keyed Chests

The Basic, Rare, and Luxury Chests all require keys, which can be obtained through rewards in the game. Each key allows you to open the respective chest once. The rewards are definitely worth it, though, as you can get high-level items.

### Daily Chest

The Daily Chest is automatically opened for you on your first login after the game's daily reset. It contains 3 Cheers used in social farming, 1 of the current Chapter Tickets, and another item that changes every day. You can see what the next few days' rewards will be by clicking on your mailbox and then the Daily Gift tab. If you lose your streak, though, these rewards will change.

Nothing needs to be done to claim these rewards, you simply have to log in. Every 7 days you will receive the Mega Reward chest. Log in every day to build your streak for even greater rewards!

*   Mega Reward- If you are a VIP player, you will get a number of Gems. If not, you will either get some Coins or some Fishing Lures.
*   Tools Reward- Axes, Pickaxes, and Stone Pickaxes. The amount you get is dependent on your level.
*   Power Hour- For one hour after you claim the daily reward, your plot crops have 50% growth time reduction and a +0.2 yield boost. You can see the amount of time remaining on this bonus from the Market on your farm when selecting a seed to buy.
*   Love Box Reward-
    *   Under level 51: [Basic Love Box](https://sfl.world/info/chests#Basic%20Love%20Box)
    *   Level 15 to 100: [Bronze Love Box](https://sfl.world/info/chests#Bronze%20Love%20Box)
    *   Level 101+: [Silver Love Box](https://sfl.world/info/chests#Silver%20Love%20Box)
*   Fishing Reward- Rods, Earthworms, and Grubs. The amount you get is dependent on your level.
*   XP Reward- An amount of XP proportional to your level. Great when all of your food is going to deliveries and pets!
*   Coins Reward- Some coins to keep your farm running smoothly. The amount you get is dependent on your level.

If you don't want to claim the reward as soon as you log in, like if you have a Power Hour and don't have a full hour to play to get the most out of it, you can close the window with the X in the upper-right corner. Then, when you are ready, open the mailbox at your farm house and use the Daily Gift tab to claim it.

In addition to all of the above, if you are a VIP player you will get an additional food item based on your level:

*   Level 1-2: Mushroom Soup
*   Level 3-5: Bumpkin Broth
*   Level 6-9: Kale Stew
*   Level 10-14: Sunflower Cake
*   Level 15-19: Orange Cake
*   Level 20-29: Parsnip Cake
*   Level 30-99: Honey Cake
*   Level 100+: Honey Cheddar

### [Pirate Chest](https://sfl.world/info/chests#Pirate%20Chest)

The Pirate Chest requires you to have a Pirate Potion equipped on your Bumpkin or any of your Farmhands. The potion can be obtained from Old Salty, as a reward for making multiple deliveries.

### [Bud Chest](https://sfl.world/info/chests#Bud%20Box)

There is an additional chest you can unlock by owning a Bud and visiting the Clubhouse in the Plaza. Every day, a specific type of Bud will unlock the chest, so check the schedule to see when you can claim yours!

## How to get keys

The Treasure Key item unlocks the Basic Chest. It can be obtained from the following places:

*   [Digging Shop](/en/mechanics/digging)\- 10x Sand and 3x Hieroglyph (one per day)
*   [Mark Shop](/en/factions/marks)\- 1000x Marks (one per day)
*   [Marketplace](/en/mechanics/marketplace) Rewards Shop- 1200 Trade Points
*   [Betty](/en/npcs/betty)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Pumpkin Pete](/en/npcs/pumpkin-pete)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Blacksmith](/en/npcs/blacksmith)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Jester](/en/npcs/jester)\- Delivery rewards

The Rare Key unlocks the Rare Chest. It can be obtained from the following places:

*   [Digging Shop](/en/mechanics/digging)\- 30x Sand and 10x Hieroglyph (one per day)
*   [Mark Shop](/en/factions/marks)\- 3000x Marks (one per day)
*   [Marketplace](/en/mechanics/marketplace) Rewards Shop- 3600 Trade Points
*   [Bert](/en/npcs/bert)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Raven](/en/npcs/raven)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Finn](/en/npcs/finn)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Cornwell](/en/npcs/cornwell)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Tywin](/en/npcs/tywin)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Victoria](/en/npcs/victoria)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Jester](/en/npcs/jester)\- Delivery rewards

The Luxury Key unlocks the Luxury Chest. It can be obtained from the following places:

*   [Digging Shop](/en/mechanics/digging)\- 100x Sand and 30x Heiroglyph (one per day)
*   [Mark Shop](/en/factions/marks)\- 10000x Marks (one per day)
*   [Marketplace](/en/mechanics/marketplace) Rewards Shop- 6000 Trade Points
*   [Cornwell](/en/npcs/cornwell)\- Delivery rewards
*   [Tywin](/en/npcs/tywin)\- Delivery rewards

The digging shop and Mark shop keys have a limit of one per day, but you can buy both in a single day if you want to.

The marketplace shop doesn't have a limit or wait period since Trade Points are harder to come by. 

 Keys may become available from special events and other sources as well, so keep an eye out!

## Chest locations

The Basic Chest is located at the left side of the Plaza, just below Tywin, and to the left of the new animal shop.

The Rare Chest is located at the Beach, just above the lower exit to the Plaza.

The Luxury Chest is also located at the Plaza, in the far upper-right corner near Bert.

The Pirate Chest is located at the Beach, to the left of the dig site, near Old Salty.

The Bud Chest is in the Bud Clubhouse in the Plaza.

## Reward Boxes

Boxes are an additional way of earning free items, with a completely different set of tiers and requirements. Unlike chests, Boxes are opened as soon as you receive them for no cost. The potential contents of each one is also visible at [https://sfl.world/info/chests](https://sfl.world/info/chests).

### Food Boxes

Primarily earned from the Cooking Pot projects that you build on your farm, you can also get one of these by helping your friend complete one of their own Pots. They contain relatively good food items, from cakes and above. No Mashed Potatoes here, unlike a certain lucky cat…

### Tool Boxes

These valuable bundles contain all kinds of useful tools for mining, fishing, and digging, and even some animal feed and medicine!

### Love Boxes

A grab bag of sorts, these could contain all sorts of items, including Coins, Gems, tools, or food. They are the only reward box that gives Love Charms, which can be used to buy… more Boxes! A Bronze Love Box will be rewarded once daily to all players who help solve the Love Island puzzle.

### Flower Boxes

Like the name suggests, these contain the valuable FLOWER token.

### Love Island Shop

In addition to the various ways of obtaining Boxes shown above, Food, Tool, and Love Boxes of all three tiers will periodically be available for sale at the Love Island Shop. Visit as often as you can to spend those Love Charms for a chance at great rewards.

### Liquidity Rewards

Another way to earn a Reward Box is through the [Wishing Well](/en/beginners/liquidity-pool); a random box (including Flower) will be granted daily to players who hold a certain amount of value. This is a fairly involved process revolving around holding a position in a liquidity pool between USDC and FLOWER on the Base network. If those terms are unfamiliar for you, refer to the linked guide.

## History

### Daily Chest

The Daily Chest was changed in December 2025. Previously, the chest was located on the top of your Farmhouse, and required a blockchain transaction to open. You could also access it from anywhere from the Earn/Rewards button near your Bumpkin's portrait. A small amount of cryptocurrency was required to process the transaction, but was hardly a large expense given the rewards.

There were three tiers to the Daily Chest, depending on the amount of expansions you had unlocked on your current island. There is no indication in the game of which “land level” you are currently on, but you can find out by looking at the "Expansion No" field at [https://sfl.world/land/](https://sfl.world/land/).

The streak bonus was every 5 days rather than every 7. Every fifth day (5, 10,15, etc.) you would get three times the amount of whatever item reward was rolled for the day.

In addition to the randomly determined item, you also received 1 of the current Chapter's Ticket for free. The 3 Cheers were provided automatically in a separate reward window. 

The old daily reward structure can be viewed [here](/game-mechanics/old_daily_rewards.png) if you are curious.

### VIP Chest

The VIP Chest was an extra bonus for active VIP members. It was removed in February 2026. It was available on the first day of a calendar month, unless that month was the beginning of a new Chapter, in which case it was available on the first Monday of that month (the same day as the beginning of the Chapter).

For the first month of a new Chapter, the item given was something that was be beneficial for the Chapter itself, such as a boost to a new resource or game mechanic. The second month reward was tools or resources, and the third was a non-boosting decoration. These rewards have been added to the new Chapter Pass system instead.
