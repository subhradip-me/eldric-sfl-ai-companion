# Leaving a Faction

## Leaving a Faction

It is possible to leave a Faction, if you believe that you will benefit somehow from being in a different one. The costs of doing this are steep, however.

*   You must sell all of your Emblems, which is a short-term benefit as you will get some FLOWER in exchange.
*   You will lose all of your Marks, so you will want to spend as many of them as you can at Eldric's shop first (but NOT on the equipment pieces).
*   You will be unable to get a Faction Pet Streak XP bonus for **six weeks** after leaving the Faction, even if you are a contributing member for the week in your new Faction.
*   All of your Faction equipment pieces that you bought with Marks will cease to function.
*   You must pay the entry fee for the new Faction.

If you still feel strongly about changing your Faction, sell all of your Emblems, then speak to the NPC in your Faction House who shows you the bonuses for owning Emblems. Your Marks will be removed automatically, so best to spend them beforehand.
