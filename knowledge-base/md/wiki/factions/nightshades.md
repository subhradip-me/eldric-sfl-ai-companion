## Intro

While there are many Npc's in the Nightshade faction house, it has been widely agreed by the community that [Mistress Raven](/en/npcs/raven) is the head of the faction, as well as a literal [Goddess](https://www.google.com/search?q=definition+of+goddess&sxsrf=AE3TifOQqo8yEJhgEasaOUVKUWdtN-r9PA%3A1760637321173) on earth.  
The Nightshades are, without a doubt, the most dark and edgy of the factions.   
It is rumored that, while not boasting it outwardly, [Cornwell](/en/npcs/cornwell) has a deep distrust of Nightshades and [Mistress Raven](/en/npcs/raven); because of their ties to Moonseekers.

  
 

## Banner Change

![](/factions/nightshades/first-look-nightshades.jpg)

faction preview image

When the faction's mechanic was only just being introduced, the community was given an early look at what the banners would look like for each faction. The initially proposed banner had a prominent eggplant instead of the signature Raven that it now boasts today.   
The collective outcry from the community members was heard by the Dev Team, so they changed the design.

## Forming the Nightshades

During a special event (need to find name for this) members were able to donate different plants and burnable resources to the ‘faction startup’ to gain faction points. At the end of this event, faction points were changed into [Faction Emblems](/en/factions/marks#emblems). Thus the Nightshades was founded!
