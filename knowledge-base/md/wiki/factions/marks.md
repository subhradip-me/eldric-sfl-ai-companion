# Marks

## Marks

Marks are the primary currency of the Kingdom, and all four Factions as a result. You will earn Marks for contributing to your [Faction Pet](/en/factions/pet), making deliveries to the [Faction Kitchen](/en/factions/kitchen), and playing [minigames](/en/mechanics/minigames) at the portal outside the Bumpkin House.

Marks can be traded in for powerful items at Eldric's shop.

The “best” or fastest way to earn Marks is generally considered to be the Kitchen, followed by the Pet, and lastly minigames. You will also earn bonus marks for placing in the top 100 members of your faction every week, or if your Faction wins the Weekly War (no matter your rank).

## Emblems

Emblems are a resource that have no direct use, but are still valuable for any Faction member. There are four different Emblems, one for each Faction, and can be bought and sold on the Marketplace under the Resources tab. **You can only trade the Emblems of the faction that you are currently in.** Some players may still have emblems from multiple factions, from before this restriction was put in place.

Once you obtain a certain number of Emblems, the amount of Marks you earn from the Pet and Kitchen will be increased. This is called earning a Rank in the Faction. The amount of Emblems needed for each Rank, and the Mark multiplier for each, can be seen here: [https://sfl.world/util/factions](https://sfl.world/util/factions)

It is not strictly necessary to obtain Emblems to participate in a Faction, but as the Rank 5/+150% bonus only requires an average of 335 Emblems, and at the average price at the time of this writing is 0.06 FLOWER per emblem, you only need roughly 20 FLOWER to obtain a substantial boost to the amount of Marks you earn.

## Faction Shop

Eldric sells a large amount of items in the Faction Shop, and it can seem overwhelming until you realize that you can only purchase ¼ of them; items not meant for your Faction are visible but not purchaseable. The items available there can be broken down into several categories:

### Decorations & Cosmetics

There are several decorative items and wearables which provide no boosts, but will demonstrate your Faction's aesthetic to any visitors to your farm.

### Food

There are three food items which can be purchased with Marks, though their cost to XP value is lower compared to [cooked](/en/mechanics/cooking) food.

### Treasure Keys

You can obtain keys for the Basic, Rare, and Luxury [Chests](/en/mechanics/chests) with Marks, though you can only get one of each per day.

### Hourglasses

You can buy hourglasses that will: 

*   **Gourmet Hourglass**\- Decrease cooking time for all kitchens by 50% (for 4 hours)- 1500 Marks
    *   Place when all cooking buildings are idle, then start new recipes
    *   The Fish Market and the Aging Shed do not count as cooking buildings for purposes of this boost
*   **Fisher's Hourglass**\- Increase your chances of catching an extra fish by 50% (for 4 hours)- 2000 Marks
*   **Blossom Hourglass**\- Reduce flower growth time by 25% (for 4 hours)- 2000 Marks
    *   Place when all flowerbeds are empty, then plant seeds
*   **Timber Hourglass**\- Reduce tree recovery time by 25% (for 4 hours)- 4000 Marks
    *   Place when all trees are ready to be chopped, then chop them
*   **Orchard Hourglass**\- Reduce fruit patch growth time by 25% (for 6 hours)- 4000 Marks
    *   Place when all fruit patches are empty, then plant new seeds. This boost will NOT affect fruits already in progress.
*   **Harvest Hourglass**\- Reduce crop growth time by 25% (for 6 hours)- 5000 Marks
    *   Place when all plots are empty, then plant seeds
*   **Ore Hourglass**\- Reduce mineral recovery time by 50% (for 3 hours)- 8000 Marks
    *   Minerals include stone, iron, gold, crimstone, and sunstone. Unless you have another boost that reduces mineral recovery time, it's unlikely you'll get more than one use out of this item.

For the time-reducing items, it is important to note that the bonuses will only take effect when a resource or product timer is initiated. For example, the Gourmet Hourglass instructs you to ensure all of your cooking buildings are idle before placing it, because any foods that are currently cooking will not have their times adjusted when the hourglass is placed. This is especially important for fruits, as time bonuses are only applied when a new fruit seed is planted, not when you harvest a grown plant and it begins replenishing.

### Faction Equipment

The real power of the Faction shop comes in the equipment pieces that you can buy, which can provide **large** bonuses if invested in properly. Each Faction's equipment have the same benefits, only the styles are different. 

*   Faction Pants (37.5K Marks)- Provides 5% bonus Marks
*   Faction Boots (37.5K Marks)- Provides 5% bonus Marks
*   Faction Helmet (56.2K Marks)- Provides 10% bonus Marks
*   Faction Sword (56.2K Marks)- Provides 10% bonus Marks
*   Faction Armor (112K Marks)- Provides 20% bonus Marks
*   Faction Crown (210K Marks)- FLOWER and Coin delivery amounts increased by 25%, and 10% bonus Marks
    *   Note: The Faction Helmet must be bought before the Crown, as it is taken from you when you make this purchase. The 10% bonus remains on the new item.
*   Faction Shield (240K Marks)- +0.25 yield to Trees and Mineral nodes
*   Faction Quiver (240K Marks)- +0.25 yield to Crops and Fruit plots
*   Faction Medallion (240K Marks)- 25% faster cooking

As you can see, these items are designed for players who put in the time and effort to contribute to their Faction. The costs may be high, but the benefits are even higher. The only item that has a prerequisite is the crown, you can buy the others in any order. However, a solid strategy is to buy them in the order listed here, each purchase increasing the speed at which you can obtain the next one.

If for some reason you decide to [leave your Faction](/en/factions/leaving) after purchasing one of these items, it will cease to function.
