# Faction Kitchen

_Not to be confused with the_ [_Kitchen_](/en/mechanics/cooking)_, a cooking building present on your Home Island_ 

# Faction Kitchen

![](/faction_kitchen.png)

The Faction kitchen is one of the best sources of Marks because of its reliability. Each week, you will be presented with three different resources, which can be anything from crops to fruits to wood and stone. You will receive Marks for every set of resources that you deliver, and the reward will decrease with every set. The amount will reset to the maximum at the next game reset, so it is up to you if you want to dump all of your stock at once or wait and give a little each day.

The Faction kitchen reward amounts are affected by Mark multipliers such as Emblem bonuses and Faction equipment. The current requirements for your faction can be viewed here: [https://sfl.world/util/factions](https://sfl.world/util/factions)

There is also a section on [https://sfl.world/land](https://sfl.world/land) (Enter your Farm ID) that will show the P2P cost of each item set, and how many Marks you've earned each day of the current week. Use these tools to best plan your strategy.
