# Bumpkins (Faction)

_The in-game term used for a player character is Bumpkin. Feed your Bumpkin to level up, equip wearables to your Bumpkin, etc. This article is about the faction also called the Bumpkins._

# Bumpkins

At home in the city or in the forest, this faction embraces nature and industry equally. 

The Bumpkin Faction house is located at the upper part of the Kingdom, near the minigame portal.
