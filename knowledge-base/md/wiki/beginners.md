## Beginner's Guide

Sunflower Land is designed as a journey — from a humble farmer planting seeds to a master explorer unlocking rare resources, crafting advanced items, and shaping the economy.

This guide gives a **high-level overview** of what players can do at each stage.

## **🌻 Early Stages**

Start small, learn the basics, and grow your farm.

##### **Crops & Fruit – Buy seeds at the market, plant & harvest for coins**

![](/beginners_guide/crops_and_fruits.png)

##### **Tools & Gathering – Craft one-time-use tools to gather resources.**

![](/beginners_guide/tools_and_gathering.png)

##### **Cooking & Levelling – Cook meals, feed your Bumpkin, and level up.**

![](/beginners_guide/cooking_and_leveling.png)

##### **Seasons - Every 7 days a new season with new crops, fish + weather events**

![](/beginners_guide/seasons.png)

##### Expanding – Grow your island and unlock new regions with more resources.

## **🐮 Mid-Game**

Once established, your farm expands into new systems and challenges.

##### **Fishing – Compost to create bait, chum the waters, and catch fish.**

![](/beginners_guide/fishing.png)

##### **Animals – Feed animals to produce eggs, milk, wool, and other materials.**

![](/beginners_guide/animals.png)

##### **Crafting – Build items needed for farming, trading, and quests.**

![](/beginners_guide/crafting.png)

##### **Flowers & Honey – Cross-breed flowers and harvest honey**

![](/beginners_guide/flowers_and_honey.png)

## **🔥 Late Game**

High-level systems for experienced farmers.

*   **Oil** – Extract oil on the Desert Island to fuel Crop Machines, Greenhouses, and speed up cooking.
*   **Obsidian** – Gather resources at Lava Pits to craft Obsidian, a high-end material for boosts.
*   **Pets** – Collect and feed pets; earn unique pet resources used in powerful shrines.
*   **Digging** – Explore the beach, solve puzzles, and uncover hidden treasures.
*   **Potion House** – Assist the Potion Master by crafting magical potions for rare prizes.

![](/beginners_guide/volcano.png)

⚡ **Tip:** The game evolves every Chapter (every 3 months) with new mechanics, SFTs, and NFTs — so farming is always growing with you.

#####   
**⚔️ Factions**

As you progress, you’ll have the chance to **join a Faction** and align yourself with one of Sunflower Land’s communities:

*   🌻 **Sunflorians**
*   🧑‍🌾 **Bumpkins**
*   🌑 **Nightshades**
*   👺 **Goblins**

Once you’ve joined, you can take part in **weekly competitions**. These events pit players against each other in farming, crafting, or resource challenges.

*   **Top performers within each faction** earn bonus rewards
