# Beginner Pack

While not affordable to everyone, these are the items that are considered most invaluable to people just starting the game.  
The list is in descending order of “power/importance"; the value of which follows suite.

* * *

## Scarecrow

Note: If Kuebiko is on your farm, Scarecrow's effect goes away. The items do not stack ( see → [FAQ: which sfts do not stack](/en/faq#do-sfts-stack) )

**Effect:**  \-15% growth time, +20% crop yield
