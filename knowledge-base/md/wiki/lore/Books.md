# Books

###### Location 1

![](/1st_book_location.jpg)

Location 1

 The 1st book is located just outside the Auctioneer, on a table behind the building.  
 written by: Cornwell

 The book reads:  
"Our brave defenders fought valiantly, but alas, we lost the great war, and the Moonseekers drove us from our  homeland. Yet, we hold onto hope, for one day we shall reclaim what was once ours. Until then, we will keep Sunflower  Land alive in our hearts and dreams, waiting for the day of our triumphant return."

###### Location 2 

![](/2nd_book_location.jpg)

Location 2

 The 2nd book is located right behind Tango, on the beach.

written by: Finnley

 The book reads:

 “A radiant fin appeared on the surface, I couldn't believe my eyes! Luckily Tango was with me, he must be my good luck  charm.”

###### Location 3

![](/3rd_book_location.jpg)

Location 3

The 3rd book is located right next to Old Salty on the desert.

written by: Old Salty

 The book reads:

 “I've crossed the seas from Treasure Island to find this bountiful land… and all for what? Some lousy relics!”

###### Location 4

![](/easter_eggs/books/book4_location.jpg)

Location 4

The 4th book is located right next to Pharaoh on the desert.

written by: Pharaoh

 The book reads:

 “An ancient secret is revealed in the desert, deciphered by the language of my ancestors.”
