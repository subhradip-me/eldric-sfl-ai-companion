# **Love Island (AKA Mystery Island, AKA Floating Island)**

Catch the heart balloon to the mysterious **Love Island** to trade in your [Love Charms](/en/mechanics/love-charms) for [$Flower](/en/home/tokens) or shop rewards. Don’t forget to solve the **Flower Puzzle** to win a bronze love box once a day!

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_1intro.png)

dreaming of that free daily love box…

## **Where to find it?**

When it’s time, go to Plaza and click the notification at bottom-left, or walk up to the balloon and click to get whisked away to Love Island!

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_1_notification.png)

(Clicking this notification will teleport you to Love Island!)

## **Flower Puzzle**

Work with other players to solve the flower puzzle and win a bronze love box once a day! The goal is to get all petals to turn Orange.

Dull color = Not enough players on the petal

Orange = Perfect amount of players on the petal

Red = Too many players on the petal

Don't worry if it takes a while to solve it. The point is to get players to work together, which can sometimes take a while. Players may be brand new and not know what to do, or experience lag or connection loss that makes it seem like they're standing on that red petal for no reason. If you can't get the reward, don't fret, as it is relatively small and you'll get another chance later.

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_2_puzzle.png)

Reward List sourced from sfl world

### **Bonus Tip!** 

Are there players idling on one petal forcing it to be stuck on red? Try **changing servers** and try your luck elsewhere. Go to Settings -> Plaza Settings -> Change Server

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_3_changeserver.png)

## **Exchange Love Charms for Flower...**

VIP players can go to the well and exchange your hard-earned Love Charms for Flower at a rate of 80 Love Charms = 1 Flower! There is a daily limit of 10,000 Love Charms (125 Flower)

**The screenshot is incorrect, the ratio was changed to 1:80 on April 8, 2026.**

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_4_well.png)

## **...Or Buy Items in the Shop!**

Save up and spend those love charms on some neat items! Boosts like the hour glass, decor like the statue and rugs, cosmetic wearables like the dino onesie and love aura. Shop items change out daily so check back often! 

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_5_shop.png)

Reward list sourced from sfl world

## **Can I check Shop Items ahead of time?**

You can use [sfl.world](https://sfl.world/) to check that day’s rewards, just enter your farm id and see the Checklist section on your Info Page.

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_checklist.png)

## _**When**_ **Does it Fly?**

In-game you can check two ways: Going directly to the Balloon itself, or your Codex. The Balloon flies three times a day for 30 minutes, and the displayed times are conveniently in your time zone.

![](/user-pages/potatofather/loveisland_when_fly.png)
