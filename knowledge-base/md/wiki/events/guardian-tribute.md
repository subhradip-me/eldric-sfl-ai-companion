# Guardian Tribute

**This feature has been disabled and the information provided here is for historical reference.**

**⚔️ Guardian Tribute**

A **Guardian Tribute** lasts for **24 hours** and occurs **daily**.

Its mechanic functions as a tribute event involving a **random resource** such as _Kale_, _Gold_, or _Crimstone_. A total of **90%** of the contributed resources are **redistributed among the top 30%** of contributors, while the remaining **10% is permanently burned 🔥**.

This feature introduces an element of **risk and strategy 📊**. Players must analyze previous contributions to secure a position within the top 30%. Overcontributing can result in losses — for example, contributing **500 Gold** when the average distribution is **300 Gold** may lead to a **net loss** of resources 💎.

* * *

**📍Where to find it ?**

Visit Kingdom > go near the bridge and visit the guardian. There is also a guardian on the left-most side of the [Love Island](/en/events/love-island), though they will offer the same deal as the one in the Kingdom. The appearance of the Guardian will change depending on the current in-game [season](/en/mechanics/seasons).
