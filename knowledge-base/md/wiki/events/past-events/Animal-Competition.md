# Animal Competition

## Intro

From [Adam](/en/user-pages/Adam)

> Howdy Bumpkins  
> 
> The animal competition is now live and we searching for the most skilful ranchers inside of Sunflower Land  
> 
> Until the 31st of January you must complete a range of animal related tasks to earn points and climb the leaderboard. At the end of the competition prizes will be given out to players, including:
> 
> *   Infernal Bullwhips,
> *   Cowbell Necklaces,
> *   Black Sheep Onesies,
> *   Merino Jumpers,
> *   Chicken Suits,
> *   Moo-vers,
> *   Black Sheeps,
> *   UFOs,
> *   50,000 SFL Prize Pool
> 
> Tasks can be completed multiple times so start farming today! (Any bounties/deliveries already completed this week have been retroactively included in your scores )
> 
> As a bonus, if you are able to beat either Craig, Spencer or Adam on the leaderboard then you will receive a special prize  
> 
> Bring it on Bumpkins 

## Description

Participants could do any of the below tasks to earn points until the end of the competition window. Based on points earned, players were rewarded with the items (mentioned above) that were designated by Adam at the beginning of the Comp.

The Competition ran from December 17th, 2024 → January 31st, 2025
