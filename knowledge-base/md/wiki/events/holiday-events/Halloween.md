# Halloween 2025

The Halloween event for 2025 ran from October 27th to November 4th. The minigame was a rogue-like dungeon crawler, where players were tasked to defeat monsters and collect relics and other powerups, culminating in a boss fight against a giant fire-breathing jack-o-lantern.

## Dungeon Tips

*   Use the sword to kill the ghosts and flower ghouls. Use the torch on the mummy and the pickaxe on the golem.
*   Only attack monsters from the sides, and run away (kite) as soon as they lose HP. Run into previous rooms to give yourself room if needed.
*   You can hit multiple monsters in one swing, so lure them into groups if possible and finish them all at once.
*   If you're running away from a monster or to reposition, switch to the torch and burn it while you move.
*   The owl puzzle has two forms:
    *   Parts grid (Sudoku)- select icons so that each image only occurs in every row and column once.
    *   Sliding puzzle- Arrange the tiles to form the image of a Bumpkin wearing the Sweet Devil set.
*   Move away from a statue as soon as you break it, it could hide a monster inside.
