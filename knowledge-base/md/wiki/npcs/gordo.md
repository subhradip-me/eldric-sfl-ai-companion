# Gordo

![](/npcs/gordo.jpg)

Gordo is a goblin who resides in the lower-right corner of the Goblin's Retreat. He, like the other goblins, requests food in exchange for FLOWER tokens. He wears a brightly-colored shirt and is one of the few Goblins who has no hair or hat, making him easy to spot from a distance.

He seems to enjoy a wide range of foods, especially cake, and may over-indulge from time to time. The other goblins are a little concerned about his health, and may have chosen his name to coerce him into eating more healthy foods.

Most of his requested foods require fruits or fish, so a fair amount of progress in the game is needed to successfully complete his requests. It is worth noting that for most of the cakes that he requests, the materials that go into them are worth more when sold on the Marketplace than he will give as a reward. During a Double Delivery Day, or if you have other boosts to delivery profits, they may become worthwhile.

## Requests

*   Banana Blast x2
*   Bumpkin Ganoush
*   Bumpkin Salad x3
*   Cabbage Cake
*   Cauliflower Burger x4
*   Chowder x2
*   Eggplant Cake
*   Goblin Brunch
*   Gumbo x2
*   Parsnip Cake
*   Power Smoothie x3
*   Wheat Cake
