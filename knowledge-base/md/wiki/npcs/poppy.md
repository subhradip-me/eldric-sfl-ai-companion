# Poppy

Poppy is the NPC who runs the Mega Bounty Board, residing in the Plaza on the lower-right side, above the cookpot goblins. She's got heaps of Chapter Tickets ready to hand out to whoever can bring her the exotic and rare items she posts on her board. If you're resourceful enough to collect them all, there's a special reward in store…

Not much else is known about Poppy other than the fact that she likes flowers, and used to accept nothing but blooms in exchange for rewards.

## Bounty Board

The only way to interact with Poppy is through the [Mega Bounty Board](/en/chapter-race/bounty-board). She does not accept deliveries, cannot be gifted flowers, and does not appear on the Weekly Chores list.
