# Stella

![](/npcs/stella.jpg)

## Stella

An enterprising entrepreneur, Stella runs the Plaza's Megastore, and will happily take all of the Tickets that you've been earning from all around the islands. What she does with all of them is unclear, but perhaps she's made a deal with Garbo to get a better rate than you have…

She is very fashion forward and can be seen demonstrating her latest wearables.

## Megastore

The only way you can interact with Stella is through the [Megastore](/en/chapter-race/megastore). She does not accept any deliveries, cannot be gifted flowers, and does not appear on the Weekly Chores list.
