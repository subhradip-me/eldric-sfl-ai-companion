# Timmy

![](/npcs/timmy.png)

## Timmy

A strange young fellow in a bear suit, Timmy resides in the upper-right corner of the Plaza, between Bert and Betty. He will request different types of fruit, drinks made with fruit, and dolls for his collection. He is a Ticket delivery NPC, meaning he will reward you with the current Chapter Ticket, and will be unavailable for the final week of each Chapter. 

Timmy appears to be the youngest resident of the Plaza; from his dressing up in a costume, always wanting sweets, and playing with toys. Some of his quotes make it seem as though he believes himself to be a bear, rather than a human, while others make it clear that he is aware he is just wearing a costume and not actually a bear.

He will not accept flowers as gifts, and does not have delivery point rewards.

Players can join Timmy in his dress-up game by purchasing the Bear Onesie in the Marketplace.

## Quotes

“Roar! I'm Timmy the bear! Gimme all the fruity goodness!”

"In a bear suit, life's a treat. Fruits are my jam, got any?"

## History

Timmy used to only request fruits and sometimes drinks made from the Smoothie Shack, but now has taken to playing with dolls and wants them for his collection. This makes him one of the most expensive NPCs to cater to, and without delivery point rewards, it is hard to justify the cost of his requests outside of Double Delivery Day.
