## Gorga

Gorga is located on [Infernos](/en/mechanics/sunstone).

She asks for 3 obsidian, and will reward you with 1 Sunstone in return.

![](/game-mechanics/infernos-sunstones/exchange-sunstone.jpg)

It is still not known why the Goblin's want Obsidian.
