# Tango

![](/npcs/tango/tango.png)

Tango resides in the center portion of the Beach, to the right of Miranda. This cheerful little guy can be found surrounded by the fruit trees he seeks. 

Tango is a daily delivery NPC and will reward you with coins for your fruit harvests. He does not accept flowers and will not reward any items.
