# Raven

The community agreed leader of the [Nightshades](/en/factions/nightshades) faction. Known to be a loner, dressing in mostly all black, and associates of Moonseekers and other “creatures of the night.” She appears to be some kind of sorceress, using delivered crops and flowers for ingredients in her magic.

She requests various things from the Bumpkins of Sunflower-Land in exchange for chapter tickets, which change almost every chapter.

She is the daughter of the [Blacksmith](/en/npcs/blacksmith). It is not known why, but Raven keeps her relation to the Blacksmith a secret from the majority of the residents of Sunflower-Land.

She prefers any flower that is purple, regardless of variety, as it is apparently the color of her soul.
