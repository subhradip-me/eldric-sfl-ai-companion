# Peggy

![](/npcs/peggy.png)

## Peggy

Peggy is an NPC residing in the Plaza, just to the left of Pumpkin' Pete. While you can see her from the path that leads left-right in front of the Megastore, you cannot reach her without walking around near Pete or the entrance to the Beach. She requests all varieties of food in exchange for Coins.

She is clearly a chef, from her Apron and the fact that she wore a Chef Hat when first moving to the Plaza. She appears to have a deep love for cooking, and is willing to reward anyone who brings her the dishes she has chosen for her menu. While she can request foods made from every cooking building, she primarily asks for simple foods made at the Firepit or Kitchen to help newer farmers build up their Coin reserves.

Peggy does not accept flowers as gifts and does not have delivery point rewards.

## Quotes

"Hello there, darling! Have you come to bring me some scrumptious delights? I can't wait to see what you've cooked up!"

"Well, butter my biscuits! Have you got those tasty treats ready for me? I'm all set for some delicious fun!"
