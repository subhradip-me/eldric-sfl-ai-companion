# Grimbly

![](/npcs/grimbly.png)

## Grimbly

One of two old goblins who have nothing better to do than sit around a cooking pot in the lower-right corner of the Plaza. Like all goblins, Grimbly will trade food for FLOWER, and is apparently so hungry that he will even eat worms.

He can be told apart from his partner Grimtooth by his Yellow Farmer Shirt. He is in charge of the construction projects that add more land to farmer's home islands, and will collect all of the resources needed before delegating construction to his goblin underlings. He also speaks about himself in the third person, a unique trait among his goblin brethren.

To fulfill his requests you will need the Firepit, Kitchen, and… the Basic Composter.
