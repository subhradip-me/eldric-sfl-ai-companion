# Finley

![](/npcs/finley.jpg)

Finley resides in the central part of the Beach, on a pier to the left of Miranda. She frequently requests Fishing Rods and various baits obtained from composters, in exchange for Chapter Tickets. As a Ticket Delivery NPC, she will be unavailable during the final weeks of each Chapter, though she will remain where she is.

She is the sister of [Finn](/en/npcs/finn), and is engaged in an ongoing competition to catch the most fish. She wears the very rare Squid Hat, possibly a creature she caught and convinced to stay on her head in exchange for food.

Finley prefers any color of Daffodil over other flowers.

## Rewards

*   25 points
    *   3x Fishing Lure
    *   Recipe- Fisher Bed
*   95 points
    *   3200x Coins
*   150 points
    *   5x Tuna
*   Every 100 points after- 5x Fishing Lure
