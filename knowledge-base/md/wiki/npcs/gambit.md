# Gambit

## Gambit

![](/npcs/gambit.jpg)

Gambit is a goblin that resides in the lower portion of the Kingdom zone, near the exit to the Plaza. He, like the other goblins, requests food in exchange for FLOWER tokens.

He wears a hat called the Knight Gambit, and has gathered the various chess-themed decorations near himself. From this and his quotes, it is clear that he enjoys gambling and gaming. Indeed, he used to be the steward of the portal for the Crops and Chickens minigame, before it was moved to the combination portal near the Bumpkin Faction House. 

Many of his requests require high-level items grown in the Greenhouse, or obtained in the Marketplace, as well as Magic Mushrooms. To be able to fulfill any of his requests, you'll need the Firepit, Kitchen, and Deli.

## Requests

*   Antipasto
*   Beetroot Blaze
*   Fried Calamari
*   Fried Tofu
*   Ocean's Olive
*   Rapid Roast
*   Shroom Syrup
*   Steamed Red Rice
*   Sushi Roll
*   Tofu Scramble
