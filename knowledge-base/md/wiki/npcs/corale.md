# Corale

![](/npcs/corale.jpg)

Corale is a mermaid that resides in the lower-left corner of the Beach zone. She will request any kind of fish for a delivery, in exchange for Coins. The amount of coins can be doubled by taking the skill Fishy Fortune. From her quotes, it's clear that she releases the fish back into the water and does not eat them.

The only flower that Corale prefers above others is the Prism Petal.

While many NPCs will reward you with wearable items that they themselves are wearing, Corale's hair, shirt, and mermaid's tail are all currently unobtainable.

## Rewards

*   45 points
    *   960 Coins
*   150 points
    *   40x Gem
    *   Recipe- Synthetic Fabric
*   320 points
    *   Pink Ponytail (wearable hair, though not the same one she has)
    *   Recipe- Kelp Fibre
*   Every 200 points- 3200 coins
