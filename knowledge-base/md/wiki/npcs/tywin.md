# Tywin

Tywin is a royal NPC who resides in the Plaza, just below the entrance to the Kingdom. He requests very expensive items such as Gold, Golden Pickaxes, Crimstone, and high sums of Coins. He is a Ticket delivery NPC, meaning he will reward you with the current Chapter Ticket, and will be unavailable for the final week of each Chapter. 

He is the favored prince of the realm, though his relationship to the reigning monarch Victoria is not clear. He would be in the Kingdom, away from the common folk, if it weren't for the treasure chest just below him. He's unable to resist the lure of the gold that might be lurking inside.

Common flowers are not fit for a prince, and Tywin won't settle for anything less than a Primula Enigma or a Celestial Frostbloom.

Tywin's impertinence has led to a [recurring joke](https://discord.com/channels/880987707214544966/1264714770817224724/1343376846074413108) at his expense where the developers of the game blame any downtime or bugs on him. 

According to the game's [source code](https://github.com/sunflower-land/sunflower-land/blob/eb757f2cbd13c5f50c64ec4dd7e5252b167f0caf/src/lib/i18n/dictionaries/en.json#L5513), one of the benefits of reaching the Grand Harvester reputation level is a “Dinner date with Tywin”
