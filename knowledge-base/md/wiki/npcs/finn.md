# Finn

## Finn

![](/npcs/finn.jpg)

Finn resides in the central area of the Beach, on a pier above [Corale](/en/npcs/corale). He requests various fish, the trash you find on other players farms, and Leather, all in exchange for Chapter Tickets. As a Ticket NPC, he will not accept deliveries during the final weeks of the Chapter.

He is the brother of [Finley](/en/npcs/finley), engaged in an ongoing fishing competition with her. He appears to use the items he requests as chum for fishing, though he requests fish directly, and may be passing those off to his sister as specimens he has caught himself…

He prefers the White and Blue Cosmos over other kinds of flowers.

## Fishing Lure

Finn is the primary source of Fishing Lures available to players, the others being chests and rewards. He will sell the player any amount of lures for 10 Gems or 100 Feathers each. Check the prices on the Marketplace and the FLOWER/Gem exchange rate to determine which is the best deal at the time.

During the period between chapters when Ticket NPCs refuse deliveries, Finn will still sell Fishing Lures.
