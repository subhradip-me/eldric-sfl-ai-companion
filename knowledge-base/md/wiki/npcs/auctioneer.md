# Auctioneer

![](/npcs/auctioneer.jpg)

## Hammerin' Harry

This hard-hitting Auctioneer resides in the right side of the Plaza, just off of the main road leading up and down. He's gathered up the finest, highest-quality items to distribute to farmers, at a premium cost, of course.

Unlike other NPCs who go by their title, the Auctioneer's name is actually Harry, his nickname being Hammerin' Harry, likely due to his habit of banging the gavel after an auction.

## Auctions

The only way to interact with Harry is when an [auction](/en/chapter-race/auctions) is live. Talking to him in his auction house will display a window detailing all of the items that will be auctioned in the upcoming weeks of the chapter, and how many are remaining. He does not accept any deliveries, cannot be gifted flowers, and does not appear for the Weekly Chores.

## Quotes

"Gather round Bumpkins, an auction is about to begin."
