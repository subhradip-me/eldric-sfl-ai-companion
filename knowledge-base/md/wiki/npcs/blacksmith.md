# Blacksmith

## Blacksmith

![](/npcs/blacksmith.jpg)

The Blacksmith resides in the upper portion of the Plaza, near the large windmill that the Mayor stands at the base of. He frequently requests Stone, Wood, Iron, and Gold, along with other materials like Sea Cucumbers, Old Bottles, and Seaweed. He will reward Coins for these items, the amount of which can be increased by 20% with the Forge-Ward Profits skill.

He does not appear to have a name, only being referred to by his title. It is unclear whether the blacksmith that runs the Workbench on your home island is the same one as in the Plaza, as while they share some elements of their appearance, the Plaza Blacksmith has much darker skin than the one on your island.

The Blacksmith is the father of [Raven](/en/npcs/raven), as revealed through a certain Telegram quest. It is not known why, but Blacksmith and Raven keep their relation a secret from the majority of the residents of Sunflower-Land. He has an important figure in the town; providing tools, craft-ables, and buildings to everyone who needs them.

The only flower that the Blacksmith prefers is the Red Carnation.
