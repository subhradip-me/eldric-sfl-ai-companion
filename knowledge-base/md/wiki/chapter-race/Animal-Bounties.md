# Animal Bounties

inside of each animal building there is an option to “sell” your animals for types of rewards (gems, coins, chapter tickets/pass points)

## Requirements

Each animal bounty has a Level requirement that the animal must meet in order to be sold for that bounty. In addition, the animal must be awake, meaning able to be fed, not asleep. If the animal is sick, you can still sell it but it will be worth half the amount listed on the bounty.

Simply follow the order in IMAGE 1, then click the animal you would like to sell.

## \# of each?

the number of each type of bounty depends on the level of the building its associated with.

*   Barn
    *   LVL 1 - Cow (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket) | Sheep (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket)
    *   LVL 2 - Cow (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket) | Sheep (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket)
    *   LVL 3 - Cow (5 coin, 1 gem, 4 chapter ticket) | Sheep (5 coin, 1 gem, 4 chapter ticket)

*   Hen House
    *   LVL 1 -  (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket)
    *   LVL 2 -  (coin, y gem, z chapter ticket)
    *   LVL 3 -  (10 coin, 2 gem, 8 chapter ticket)

5 Chapter Pass points will be awarded per Ticket for every Chapter Ticket bounty.

## Final notes

*   Bounties reset once every week.
*   You can view Ticket bounties for all animal types in the Chapter tab of your Codex at any time. Coin or Gem bounties are not displayed there, and the Pass points are not shown either.
*   Each bounty can only be done once, when it has been completed the button will darken.
