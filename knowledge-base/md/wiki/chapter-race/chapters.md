# Chapter Race

The ‘chapter race' is a term that refers to the competition that takes place over every 3-month period in the game. During this time farmers collect Chapter Tickets and Chapter Points, and use them to earn fabulous prizes.

## How do I get tickets and points?

The methods of getting chapter tickets include:

*   Daily Reward- 1 Ticket per player per day
*   Animal Bounties- Ticket amounts vary based on animal level, plus 5 track points per ticket
*   Mega Bounty Board- Ticket amounts vary based on item rarity, plus 5 track points per ticket
*   Deliveries- Ticket amounts vary based on delivery value, plus 5 track points per ticket. Coin and FLOWER deliveries also award 10 track points per delivery.
*   Weekly Chores- Ticket amounts vary based on chore complexity, plus 3 track points per ticket

There are several different items and wearables that can boost your ticket-earning potential, some are rewarded in the Chapter Track, and some are available at Stella's Megastore. Getting as many of these as you can afford, and making the most of Double Delivery Day, is the key to maximizing your earnings.

## Why should I collect chapter tickets?

Chapter Tickets are used to buy entries into the weekly raffles; 100 prizes are handed out every week, and you can potentially get something for only a single entry!

You can also save up your tickets to buy powerful items at Stella's Megastore.

Finally, tickets are used to bid on valuable items in the Auctions that take place during a special week near the end of each Chapter.

While earning tickets, you will also get chapter (track) points, which are used to unlock unique rewards more reliably. Even if you gamble all your tickets on a raffle and lose, you'll still have your points.

## USE IT OR LOSE IT!!!

The tickets in every chapter are only good during the chapter they are obtained from. At the end of the chapter tickets become ‘nearly worthless’. The only place the can be used/removed from inventory is by giving them to [Garbo](/en/npcs/garbo); who will purchase them from you for a very cheap coin price (usually 0.1 coins each).

So in conclusion; use your tickets before the end of the chapter or you will lose the value.
