# Mega Bounty Board

“Gather the resources & exchange them for rewards before time runs out!”

The Mega Bounty Board is one of the various ways for players to earn Chapter Tickets and Chapter Points. It is also one of the most available, catering to multiple different types of players. Whether you choose to fish, craft, or grow flowers, you will likely find something here that you can tackle.

Each bounty is worth the stated amount of Chapter Tickets, as well as three times as many points toward the Chapter Pass.

The items requested on the board change every week on Monday, at the same time as a new season begins. You can view the current lineup from anywhere in your Codex, including the value of the reward and whether or not you have any of that item in your inventory, but you must travel to the lower-right side of the Plaza and speak with Poppy to actually turn items in for the reward.

## Flower Bounties

Five different [flowers](/en/mechanics/flowers) will be requested among all of those currently available. There may be some you have never grown before, so use this as an opportunity to experiment with new combinations. Also do the math to ensure you will actually be able to grow a particular flower before the week ends altogether, unless you are willing to use some of that valuable Obsidian…

## Fish Bounties

Any five of the various aquatic treasures will be up for rewards. Fish that are not in season are possible candidates, so make sure you have some fish products on hand to get the one you need through a guaranteed catch.

## Crustacean Bounties

Craft some water traps to get these valuable creatures. These are usually worth quite a bit more than fish.

## Exotic Bounties

This category currently consists of the exotic crops that can be purchased with Potion Tickets. You can also get them from the Magic Bean, also for sale in the potion minigame store.

## Giant Fruit Bounties

Round up your friends to help grow these special exotic fruits. Each fruit can be turned in once per week. Extras can be sold for coins or given to certain NPCs for their own deliveries.

## Doll Bounties

Five of those new-fangled dolls will be up for redemption, too. Pick which ones you can afford to make and turn them in for quite a few rewards!

## Obsidian Bounties

Each of the three bounties in this category require one Obsidian. Since they each have differing reward amounts and all only require one, it is easy to see which should be redeemed first…

## Bounty Bonus

In the event that you are an extremely capable player and can complete all of the possible bounties, you can claim an additional large reward. This is a tall order for even the most experienced players, but it is a good goal to work toward! 

## History

The bounty board used to include items that you could get from digging or fishing like Old Bottles, Sand, and Sea Cucumbers. These were removed in the Paw Prints chapter, along with the Moon Berries, and Marks earned from the Kingdom. Previously multiple copies of each Giant Fruit could be turned in for rewards in a single week.

### Limited Marketplace

Previously to the Craps & Traps chapter, it was possible to buy and sell a large selection of items requested on the bounty board each week. Now that flowers can be grown instantly with Obsidian and guaranteed catch fishing has been expanded, the limited section has been removed.
