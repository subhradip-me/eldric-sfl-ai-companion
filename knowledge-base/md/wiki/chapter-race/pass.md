# Chapter Pass

![chapterdashboard.png](/chapter-mechanics/chapterdashboard.png)

### AKA Chapter Track, Battle Pass, Chapter Rewards

The newest way to earn rewards in the game, the Chapter Pass is open to all players, and is the primary form of progression and rewards in the new system. Earn points for doing things that you already do, and benefit greatly!

### Reward Tracks

The two tracks are accessible in the middle of the new Chapter Dashboard, accessed by the green journal icon near your bumpkin's portrait.

As you earn chapter points, you will unlock rewards on the tracks you have access to. There are two, one for VIP players and one for all players. If you unlock a rank as a free player, then decide to purchase a VIP membership, you will get the reward from every VIP rank you have already unlocked..

Chapter Points are earned from several different sources:

*   Ticket Deliveries- In addition to Chapter Tickets, your favorite NPCs will give you **5 points per ticket** in their reward.
*   Coin Deliveries- A flat **10 points** will be added to every delivery for Coins, no matter the reward amount.
*   Bounty Board- Earn **3 points per ticket** you earn on each bounty.
*   Animal Bounties- Each animal is worth **3 points per ticket** when sold.
*   Weekly Chores- Chores grant **3 points per ticket** also.

Since point amounts are tied to ticket amounts, Double Delivery Day will only increase the point rewards from Ticket Deliveries, not Coin Deliveries.

### Claiming Rewards

The game does not notify you when you have earned enough points to unlock a new rank and reward. Check the dashboard periodically to see what you're eligible for. The rank icon in the upper-right of the window shows your progress to the next rank, while clicking on a reward that you haven't earned yet will show the amount of points overall for the entire chapter.

### Social Rewards

Twitter rewards, referral links, Discord and Telegram links have all been moved to the Earn window accessed in the lower-right of this screen.

### Megastore, Auctions, Raffles

You can view the items available in Stella's pared-down [Megastore](/en/chapter-race/megastore) at the button in the upper-right, though you still need to visit her in the Plaza to actually buy something with Chapter Tickets.  
Similarly, you can view upcoming [auctions](/en/chapter-race/auctions), but must travel to the Plaza to place your bids once they begin.  
But, you can enter the [weekly raffles](/en/chapter-race/raffles) from home or anywhere, and add additional entries if you want.
