# Stella's Megastore

If you don't feel like trying your luck in a raffle, you can always save up your Chapter Tickets and trade them to Stella for some powerful prizes. She also carries some items that will boost your overall ticket-earning potential, and will accept Digging artifacts too. Simply gather the required amount of resource and visit her in the Plaza to purchase your desired item. 

If an item has a boost, a lightning bolt icon will indicate that in the menu. Most items are limited to one per player. Items that cost FLOWER are discounted by 50% for VIP players (yes, that means the items in the screenshot above cost 100 and 200 FLOWER respectively for free players).

Some items cannot be traded on the Marketplace even after the chapter ends, these are clearly marked in their description. These items are usually also monuments, meaning they must be placed on your farm and your friends help you build them, just like with Giant Fruits and other special items.

You can also view the current Chapter Pass rewards from her window. You can view the items available from your Chapter Dashboard anywhere in the game, but must travel to the Plaza to buy something.

## History

With the advent of the Chapter Pass rewards system, Stella's Megastore became a lot less mega. Her offerings used to be divided up into the four-tier layout that is currently used for the Event Shop during holiday events; Basic, Rare, Epic, and Mega items. In order to buy items from the higher tiers, you needed to have have bought at least four items in the tier below it. After unlocking a tier, you could buy any item in it.

The lineup of items changed every Chapter, but there were some recurring types of items. The basic tier contained decorations and ticket-boosting items, while the rare tier had more useful items for general gameplay. The last two tiers had expensive, powerful items that were highly sought after once the Chapter ended. 

Most items required Tickets to redeem, but a few cost FLOWER, and others required the current Artifact from the Beach Dig Site. Some items had additional costs on top just tickets. For example, the Double Bed offered during Chapter 11 not only required 1250 Bracelets, but also 5000 Wool and 2500 Gems. 

The three treasure Keys were available for purchase, one in each of the first three tiers. As with the other ways to obtain these items, you could only get one per day, but instead of another one being available at game reset, you actually had to wait 24 hours for the item to "cool down".

Bronze, Silver, and Gold Flower Boxes were also available. The cooldown for those items was 7 days (168 hours), down to the minute, so planning was needed if players wanted to buy multiples.
