# Chapter Raffle

![](/chapter-mechanics/raffles.png)

### Chapter Raffles

Wondering what to do with all those Chapter Tickets? Try your luck in a little game! Every week during a chapter, a certain set of items will be put up for raffle. You can buy entries using Chapter Tickets, or other resources specified in the raffle details. Unlike auctions, where the richest players always win, raffles allow for newer players to participate as well. Even though you may only have 10 entries and that top-level player has 1,000, you still stand a chance to win something!

To enter a raffle, visit the Auctioneer's house in the Plaza and tap on the chest labeled Prizes. Or, you can just access the current raffle in your Chapter Dashboard from anywhere in the game.

You can also view the results of past raffles from the Plaza, including the amount of entries the winners had submitted. Don't think that your single entry isn't worth anything.

Remember that you can only win **one prize per raffle**, no matter how many entries you submit.

### Earning Entries

Most raffles will accept two forms of entry: Chapter Tickets and Chapter _Raffle_ Tickets. Standard tickets are earned from deliveries and bounties, while Raffle Tickets are currently earned only from the Chapter Pass rewards.

*   Chapter Tickets are worth **10 entries**
*   Raffle Tickets are worth **1 entry**

The reason for two different forms of entry is to allow players to keep their standard tickets and try for the high-level Megastore items while also being able to participate in the raffles. You cannot remove entries from a raffle once you have submitted them, but you can add more up until the end date.

### Prize Tickets

While the Goblin Raffle may have officially ended, its spirit lives on with the Chapter Raffles. Every month of the chapter will have a special raffle for a Bud NFT and other fantastic prizes, which will accept Prize Tickets as forms of entry.

*   Prize Tickets are worth **200 entries** in the Bud raffle only
*   Chapter Tickets and Raffle Tickets are worth their normal amount of entries (if they are accepted)

### Chances of Winning

While the raffle mechanic is still brand new and no statistics are available, the list of prizes for each raffle is encouraging. Unlike an auction, 100 prizes will be handed out per week, all of which are useful in some way. Even if you don't get the headliner prize, you could still win some Gems or a high-tier Food Box.

If you don't win anything in the previous week's auction, you will receive 100 coins as a consolation prize.
